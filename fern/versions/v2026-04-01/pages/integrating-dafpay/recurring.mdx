---
title: "Recurring Grants"
hidden: false
---

When enabled, a donor can ask their DAF provider to send a monthly grant.

A recurring success contains `recurringGrantIntent` instead of `grantIntent`. Call [Create Recurring Grant](/api/recurring-grants/create), not Create Grant.

The DAF provider owns the schedule. Chariot records the initial setup but may not receive a new Grant record for each later payment or notice when the donor changes or cancels the schedule.

Model the initial record as the start of a recurring arrangement, not as proof of future payments. Treat each received Donation as its own gift and reconcile it independently.

Do not promise nonprofits a complete recurring schedule unless your own data source can support it.
