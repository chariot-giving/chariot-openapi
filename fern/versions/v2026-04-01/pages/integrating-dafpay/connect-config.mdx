Decide how DAFpay should behave before you build. Chariot applies the agreed configuration to your sandbox Connects before QA and to production Connects before launch.

Decide each of the following and send us your choices:

- which donor details DAFpay displays and requires;
- whether donors can request monthly Grants;
- how to handle providers DAFpay cannot connect to directly;
- whether your platform charges a fee and whether donors may cover it;
- where DAFpay appears across your form types; and
- how DAFpay gifts move into connected CRMs and receive hard or soft credit.

Email your configuration to [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) before you build. Your sandbox test URL, test instructions, and launch date come later with the production-access request.

## Change the configuration later

Email [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) and state whether the change should apply to sandbox, production, or both. Treat a material change to enablement, fees, recurring behavior, unintegrated-provider behavior, or CRM credit as a new release that needs testing.
