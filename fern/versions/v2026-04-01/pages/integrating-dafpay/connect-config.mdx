Agree on the production behavior before you build. Ask [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) to apply the same configuration in sandbox so your test covers the real flow.

Decide:

- which donor details DAFpay collects or receives from your form;
- whether monthly recurring grants are enabled;
- what happens when the donor's DAF provider is not supported;
- whether the unsupported-provider confirmation closes automatically;
- which fees can be added and whether the donor may cover them;
- where DAFpay appears across standard donations, tickets, registrations, merchandise, sponsorships, and other form types; and
- whether DAFpay gifts pass through your CRM integrations and receive hard or soft credit.

Send these decisions to [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) in the implementation questionnaire. Chariot will use them to configure your sandbox Connects before QA and your production Connects before launch.
