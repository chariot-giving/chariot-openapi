Once you have completed all the steps on the [Integration Checklist](/guides/dafpay/integrating-dafpay/checklist), email `integrations@givechariot.com` to request production API keys.

Copy the block below, fill in every line, and send it as the body of that email. The checklist covers what our QA would otherwise find in sandbox, and a complete request turns around faster.

## The request

```text
Organization:
Platform type: fundraising platform / nonprofit running its own form
Target go-live date:

CONNECT CONFIGURATION
Donor fields the modal shows:
Donor fields the modal requires:
Monthly recurring gifts: enabled / disabled
Donors on an unconnected DAF provider: record the grant intent / return to our form

SANDBOX TEST FORM
Link:

QA CHECKLIST (sandbox, confirm each line)
[ ] Successful donation: a sandbox gift completes end to end and the grant appears in our records
[ ] On-donation data: donor name, email, phone and address arrive on the donation, and our metadata (campaign, source, form ID, designation) comes through
[ ] Button placement: the DAFpay button sits at the top of the form, above the gift amount and ahead of our contact fields
[ ] Change amount in modal: when a donor changes the amount inside the DAFpay window, we record the amount from CHARIOT_SUCCESS rather than the one from our form
[ ] Confirmation redirect: the donor lands on our confirmation page once the window closes, with a loading screen in between, and the page does not say the gift is being processed or promise a tax receipt
[ ] Unintegrated grants: a donor on an unconnected provider ends up where our configuration says, and if we record intents, the confirmation page shows the tracking ID with its "Tracking ID:" prefix and a way to copy it, our EIN, the organization name, and a link to the provider
[ ] Recurring, if enabled: a monthly intent goes to Create Recurring Grant and records frequency MONTHLY
[ ] Fees, if we collect them: applicationFeeAmount matches what we charge, final amounts are whole dollars, and the tip option is suppressed when DAFpay is selected

BROWSERS (confirm each, mobile and desktop)
[ ] Chrome
[ ] Safari
[ ] Firefox

FUNDRAISING PLATFORMS ONLY
Nonprofit we can send the production test donation to:
Recording of a nonprofit turning DAFpay on in our platform:
Help article link:

LAUNCH PLAN

Where the co-branded Chariot signup link lives on your site:
```

## Notes on a few lines

**The QA and browser lines are confirmations, not artifacts.** No recording or screenshot needed. Mark anything that doesn't apply on its line rather than deleting it. Check each browser on mobile and desktop — popup handling and the redirect back to your confirmation page are where they differ.

**The recording covers nonprofit onboarding.** Show how a nonprofit turns DAFpay on in your platform, the way one of your customers would. The sandbox test form already covers the donation.

**The launch plan is the part people underestimate.** On by default means backfilling every nonprofit you already have plus a path for each new one. Opt-in means something has to check the nonprofit has its Chariot Deposit Account before the button goes live. Write down how each actually happens.

We also schedule a go-live review before issuing keys, covering your launch date, your help article, and what each side announces.

**The help article answers four questions** nonprofits ask us constantly and only you can answer:

* How does a nonprofit enable DAFpay?
* Is it per form or account-wide?
* Where does the nonprofit claim its Chariot Deposit Account?
* Do DAFpay gifts sync to their CRM, and how?

Send it once, published for your customers. Later requests can point at the same article.
