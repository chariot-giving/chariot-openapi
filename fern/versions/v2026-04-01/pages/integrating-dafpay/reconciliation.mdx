DAFpay never handles the funds. Each DAF provider pays the nonprofit directly, by transfer or mailed check, and stamps the payment with identifiers you can match back to the grant.

| Identifier | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Tracking ID | A 10-digit code from DAFpay, sent with the grant request. Appears in the provider's data as a `Tracking ID:` string in the grant note or purpose. |
| External Grant ID | The provider's own grant identifier, returned after a successful initiation. Format varies; usually shows up as "Grant ID". |

Payments also carry a "Gift via DAFpay" callout in the memo.

<Warning title="Make both searchable">
Display `trackingId` and `externalGrantId` as searchable fields on your transactions dashboard. Without them nobody can match a payment to a grant.
</Warning>

To show the provider's name, like Fidelity Charitable, pass the grant's `fundId` to [Get DAF](/api/donor-advised-funds/get).

<Warning title="Not every provider sends a tracking ID">
Fall back to the external grant ID, then to the donor's fund name, amount and other grant details.
</Warning>

## What to persist

One row per checkout, on your side, is what makes reconciliation possible later.

| Field | When you get it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your transaction ID | At checkout | Your key, not ours |
| `nonprofit_id` | At checkout | Which of your customers this belongs to |
| `connect_id` (CID) | At nonprofit onboarding | Proves which connect the session ran on |
| `workflowSessionId` | `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` | Ties the browser session to the capture |
| Grant `id` | Create Grant response | Stable |
| `donationId` | Asynchronously, from Get Grant | Absent at first; retry with backoff |
| Grant status | `grant.updated` webhook | `Initiated` until the nonprofit has the money |
| Tracking ID, external grant ID | On the grant | What appears on the provider's remittance |

Reconcile on the grant ID and the donation ID. Don't use the tracking ID as a key, since about 8% of grants don't have one.
