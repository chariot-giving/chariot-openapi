Everything on the overview applies to every DAFpay integration. This page picks your path.

## Accept Grant Requests

For anyone putting DAFpay in front of donors.

| If you want to | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Add DAFpay to a form hosted by a fundraising platform | [Find your platform](https://www.givechariot.com/dafpay-setup#integration-search) |
| Build directly against the API, for one organization or many | [Build a custom integration](/guides/dafpay/accept/api) |

If your donation form is hosted by a fundraising platform that already supports DAFpay, you do not need this guide. Enable it through that platform and Chariot handles the rest.
