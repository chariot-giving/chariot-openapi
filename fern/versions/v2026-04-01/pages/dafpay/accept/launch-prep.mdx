## Testing and QA

Before you request production access, complete this test matrix in sandbox.

### Sandbox Credentials

Point your development and staging environments at `https://sandboxapi.givechariot.com` with your `sk_test_` key and your `test_` Connect ID. Every integrated provider in the sandbox list is a mock, and the same login works for all of them:

```text
username: good-user
password: password123
```

No real money moves. Sandbox-only endpoints let you drive any Grant to a terminal state so you can test your received-funds handling end to end. There is no sandbox dashboard for platforms, so use these instead of marking gifts received by hand:

```text
POST https://sandboxapi.givechariot.com/v1/simulations/grants/{id}/complete
POST https://sandboxapi.givechariot.com/v1/simulations/grants/{id}/cancel
```

Send your sandbox key in the `Authorization` header. Use the resulting events to confirm your integration retrieves the changed record and updates its own transaction.

### Test Tenant Routing

If you serve many nonprofits, open two of their forms at the same time and confirm each one loads its own Connect ID and produces a Grant for the intended nonprofit. Confirm DAFpay is absent or disabled for a nonprofit that has not enabled it.

This catches hardcoded, cached, fallback, and process-wide Connect IDs, which send a donor's gift to a different nonprofit.

### Test the Donor Flow

- Complete a one-time Grant.
- Complete a recurring Grant, if enabled.
- Test every exit reason your interface handles.
- Test an unintegrated DAF provider, if supported.
- Confirm DAFpay opens within one second of the donor's click.
- Test duplicate clicks and [Create Grant](/api/grants/create) retries, and confirm only one Grant is created.
- Verify webhook signatures and repeated delivery.
- Test with popup blocking enabled, and confirm your page explains what to do if DAFpay cannot open.

If DAFpay fails to open, the cause is almost always work happening between the click and the window: see [Prevent Blocked Popups](/guides/dafpay/integrate-dafpay/adding-dafpay#prevent-blocked-popups).

### Test Browsers and Devices

DAFpay opens in a frame or popup depending on the browser and screen size. Test the full donor flow on:

- Chrome, Safari, and Firefox on desktop.
- Safari and Chrome on iPhone.
- Chrome on Android.
- Every in-app or embedded browser your platform supports.

Sandbox does not validate real settlement or remittance reconciliation. Confirm those workflows after launch with Chariot and a participating nonprofit.

## Troubleshooting

What to check when a DAFpay integration misbehaves.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="**The Popup Is Blocked on Mobile**">
DAFpay must open within one second of the donor's click, with no call to your server in between. See [Prevent Blocked Popups](/guides/dafpay/integrate-dafpay/adding-dafpay#prevent-blocked-popups).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**DAFpay Completed, but No Grant Exists**">
Confirm that your server received `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` and called [Create Grant](/api/grants/create) within 15 minutes. Preserve the workflow session, Connect ID, nonprofit, and platform transaction ID for investigation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**The Wrong Nonprofit Received the Grant**">
Treat this as a tenant-routing incident. Stop the affected rollout, preserve all identifiers and timestamps, and contact [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) immediately.

Check for a hardcoded, cached, fallback, last-seen, or process-wide Connect ID.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Create Grant Returned an Error**">
Look up the status code in the response table on [Create the Grant](/guides/dafpay/integrate-dafpay/creating-grants#retry-safely).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Webhook Events Are Missing**">
Confirm the event type, environment, endpoint, and signature code. Use [List Events](/api/events/list) to determine whether Chariot created the event or delivery failed. Keep a recovery process for state that must not be missed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**The Button Does Not Render or Open**">
If the button does not render or clicking it does nothing:

1. Check the browser console for a blocked script, frame, font, style, or image.
2. Check the Network panel for requests blocked to `cdn.givechariot.com` or `secure.dafpay.com`.
3. Test with the same response headers used in production.
4. Check both enforced and report-only policies.

When CSP is delivered through a `<meta http-equiv="Content-Security-Policy">` tag, `frame-ancestors`, `report-uri`, `report-to`, and `sandbox` are ignored. Use a response header when possible.

The directives DAFpay needs are on [Install DAFpay](/guides/dafpay/integrate-dafpay/adding-dafpay#content-security-policy).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Go Live

Request production access once you have completed the [integration checklist](/guides/dafpay/integrate-dafpay/api#integration-checklist). Chariot issues production keys after reviewing your sandbox integration, so you do not need to deploy to production first.

### Request Production Access

Email your Chariot contact or [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) with:

- Sandbox form URL and how to test it
- Production domains
- Webhook endpoint and the events you subscribe to
- Which of recurring, unintegrated providers and platform fees you support
- Whether the modal shows Address and Note, and whether each is hidden, optional, or required. Donor name and email are always required
- Screenshots or a recording of your loading, success, exit and confirmation states
- Screenshots or a recording of the nonprofit enable and disable setting, and the data you store on each gift

If you serve many nonprofits, also send:

- Nonprofit onboarding and enablement flow
- Launch-backfill plan
- Help article, launch announcement and co-branded Chariot onboarding link
- Nonprofit and live form Chariot can use for the production test
- Target launch date

When your agreement includes revenue share, also send billing contacts, ACH information and tax documentation.

### Validate the First Live Gift

Production has no mock providers, so a live test gift has to come from a real donor-advised fund. Chariot can make the gift if nobody on your side holds a DAF.

- [ ] The form used the intended production Connect ID.
- [ ] Exactly one Grant was created.
- [ ] Your record stores the workflow session and Grant ID.
- [ ] Loading, confirmation and email copy read correctly.
- [ ] The gift can be found and reconciled.
- [ ] CRM sync and hard or soft credit match the agreed rules.
