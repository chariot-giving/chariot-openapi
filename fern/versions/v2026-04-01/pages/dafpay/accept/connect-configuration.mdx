<Note>
  **For everyone.** These settings apply whether Chariot provisions your Connect or you create one per nonprofit.
</Note>

Some of how DAFpay behaves is decided on the Connect, not in your code. Settle these before you build, because they change what your form has to collect and which flows you have to handle.

## What Chariot sets for you

Ask your Chariot contact to confirm or change any of these on your sandbox and production Connects. They are not settable through the API.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Fields the modal collects | Which donor details DAFpay asks for, and which of them are required. |
| Return donor information | Whether DAFpay may pull the donor's details from their DAF provider. With it off, the provider is never asked and the donor fills in anything your form did not pass. This is what makes express checkout work. |
| Recurrence toggle | Whether the donor can switch between one time and monthly inside the modal. With it off, the frequency is whatever you passed. |
| Unintegrated grants | Whether a donor whose provider is not connected to DAFpay can record an intent, or is returned to your form to pick another payment method. |
| Unintegrated confirmation screen | Whether Chariot shows the donor their tracking ID and provider link, or leaves that to your own confirmation page. |

## When the settings do not hold

If you are a fundraising platform and a gift routes through DAFpay Network, the effective configuration is overridden so every donor sees the same DAFpay experience, whatever your Connect says:

- first name, last name and email are collected **and required**, even if you removed them; and
- donor-information return is **on**, even if you turned it off.

Plan for it rather than around it. A form that does not collect an email will still see email required in the modal, and an express-checkout flow you disabled will still pull donor details from the provider.

This does not apply to a nonprofit integrating its own form. The override only runs for gifts made through a fundraising application, so if Chariot provisions your Connect, the settings above hold as configured.

Ask Chariot whether your application routes through DAFpay Network, and under what conditions.

## Checking the rest

The unintegrated confirmation screen is worth checking explicitly. If Chariot's screen is off and your page does not show the tracking ID, the donor leaves with no way to complete the gift. If both are on, they see two confirmations.

## Retiring a Connect

There is no published endpoint for archiving or deactivating a Connect. When a nonprofit leaves your platform, stop rendering DAFpay for them and ask Chariot to retire the Connect.

Keep your own record of which nonprofits you have created Connects for. There is no published way to list everything you have created, so your records are the worklist for a backfill rather than anything you can reconcile against.

## Before you start building

Confirm, in writing:

- Which donor fields the modal collects, and which are required.
- Whether the donor-information lookup is on.
- Whether recurring is on, and whether the donor may toggle it.
- Whether unintegrated grants are on, and who shows the confirmation.

Sandbox and production are configured separately, so confirm both. A sandbox Connect that behaves differently from production will pass QA and fail at launch.
