## Where DAFpay Funds Arrive

DAFpay submits the request but does not hold the donor's DAF funds. Gifts reach you the way that provider normally pays: electronically into your Chariot account for providers Chariot is connected to, and directly to your organization by ACH or check for the rest.

Gifts reach the nonprofit one of three ways:

| Route | When it applies | How the funds arrive | Grant marked Completed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electronically to Chariot Deposit Account | The provider pays through one of Chariot's disbursement partners or was linked to their Chariot account by the nonprofit | Electronically into the nonprofit's Chariot account | Automatically |
| Outside of Chariot | Unlinked provider | ACH or check straight to the nonprofit | When the nonprofit marks it Received |
| DAFpay Network | Your platform uses the DAFpay Network and the nonprofit has not claimed its Chariot account. Some platforms route every gift this way. Never for recurring gifts. | The DAFpay Network pays the nonprofit, less fees: electronically if it has or claims a Chariot account, otherwise by check after about five business days | Automatically if paid electronically; when the nonprofit marks it Received if paid by check |

Timing varies by route and provider, from a few days to several weeks.

On a gift the provider pays you outside of Chariot, their own remittance carries the DAFpay identifiers. These examples show where the Tracking ID appears in the memo, usually as "Tracking ID {id} - Gift through DAFpay". Wording varies by provider. On the DAFpay Network route the provider pays the network, so match on the network's payout instead.
