<Note title="For Nonprofits">
  This page is for nonprofits integrating their own donation form. If you are a fundraising platform offering DAFpay across many nonprofits' forms, see [Displaying DAFpay Gifts](/guides/dafpay/accept/displaying-gifts) instead.

  Donations and Deposits are scoped to an organization. A platform API key is bound to a fundraising application rather than to an organization, so these calls return a `401`. A platform can create a donation subscription and it will be accepted, but it will never deliver.
</Note>

Every Grant has a Donation, but not every Donation has a Grant. A donor who gives from their provider's own app, without ever touching your form, produces a Donation and no Grant. That only happens if Chariot sees the gift, through a connected source or funds landing in your Chariot account.

Building your gift processing around Donations allows you to use the same workflow for DAFpay gifts and gifts from your other connected payment sources.

## Find the Donation

A donation record is created after the Grant. Once the Donation exists, its ID is added to the corresponding grant. This happens asynchronously, so `donationId` may not be present if you retrieve the Grant immediately.

Store `donationId` on your record as soon as the Grant returns it. It is the link that is present for every provider, and reading it once is cheaper than looking the Grant up again later.

Most donation IDs appear within a second or two. A small number take several seconds, and occasionally one takes considerably longer, so retrieve the Grant again with a short backoff rather than calling once and treating the donation as missing.

## What Happens Next

Once the Donation exists, it behaves like any other gift in Chariot. [Donation Status](/guides/processing/donation-status) covers `payment_status`, settlement timestamps, retrieving donations, and writing your own status back.

Two things are worth knowing before you get there. A DAFpay gift paid directly to you stays incoming until someone confirms receipt, and the Grant stays `Initiated` alongside it. Both can sit there for a long time, so treat them as a resting state rather than a failure.

## Matching a Payment That Arrives Outside Chariot

| Identifier | Where to find it | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Donation ID | Donation `id`, or the Grant's `donationId` | Update the same gift in your CRM. |
| DAFpay Tracking ID | Grant `trackingId`, or Donation `initiation.dafpay_tracking_id` | Match the provider's remittance to the initiated gift. |
| Provider Grant ID | Grant `externalGrantId` | Match the provider's own grant record when the tracking ID is absent. |
| Your transaction ID | `initiation.dafpay_metadata` | Find the original form submission. |

If no identifier appears with the payment, compare the donor's fund name, amount, date, and designation, and review an uncertain match before marking the Donation received. Make the tracking ID and provider Grant ID searchable in your own tools. Some providers batch several gifts into one payment, so one payment can match more than one Donation.
