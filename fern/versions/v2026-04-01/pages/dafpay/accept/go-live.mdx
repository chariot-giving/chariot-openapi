Once you have completed the [integration checklist](/guides/dafpay/accept/checklist), request production access.

## Request production access

Email your Chariot contact or [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) with:

- your technical and operational contacts;
- your sandbox form URL and how to test it;
- your production domains;
- your webhook endpoint and the events you subscribe to;
- which of recurring, unintegrated providers and platform fees you support; and
- screenshots or a recording of your loading, success, exit and confirmation states.

If you serve many nonprofits, also send:

- your nonprofit onboarding and enablement flow;
- your launch-backfill plan;
- the help article and co-branded Chariot onboarding link your nonprofits will see;
- the nonprofit and live form Chariot can use for the production test; and
- your target launch date.

When your agreement includes revenue share, also send billing contacts, ACH information and tax documentation.

## After approval

1. Store the production API key in your secret manager.
2. Switch to `https://api.givechariot.com`.
3. Use production Connect IDs only.
4. Create production event subscriptions. Sandbox subscriptions do not carry over.

Production credentials do not mean DAFpay is working end to end. Validate a live gift before you open it to donors.

## Validate the first live gift

Production has no mock providers, so a live test gift has to come from a real donor-advised fund. Arrange who is giving before you schedule the test — Chariot can make the gift if nobody on your side holds a DAF.

- [ ] The form used the intended production Connect ID.
- [ ] Exactly one Grant was created.
- [ ] Your record stores the workflow session and Grant ID.
- [ ] Loading, confirmation and email copy read correctly.
- [ ] The gift can be found and reconciled.
- [ ] CRM sync and hard or soft credit match the agreed rules.
