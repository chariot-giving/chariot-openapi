## Connects and architecture

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="**How many Connects does our platform need?**">
One per nonprofit. Store each Connect ID on the corresponding tenant record.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**When should we create a Connect?**">
When the nonprofit enables DAFpay. Do not create it during donor checkout. Backfill existing nonprofits using a restartable batch job.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Can the Connect ID appear in browser code?**">
Yes. The Connect ID initializes the component. The API key must remain on your server.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Can we enable DAFpay before the nonprofit claims its Chariot account?**">
Yes. A nonprofit that has not completed onboarding and verification with Chariot is paid through the DAFpay Network, so DAFpay can accept gifts for any eligible 501(c)(3).

Claiming still matters: until the nonprofit claims and verifies its Chariot account, it has no dashboard to reconcile gifts in, and funds reach it through the DAFpay Network rather than directly. Confirm the onboarding experience you want with Chariot before launch.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Checkout and Grant creation

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="**What does `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` mean?**">
The donor authorized the DAF request. Your server must still create the Grant. It does not mean the nonprofit received the money.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Can a donor change their donation amount in the modal?**">
Yes, for three reasons:

1. Donors who can see their fund balance often choose to give more.
2. Some DAF providers set a minimum grant amount.
3. The amount cannot exceed the fund balance.

Use the amount returned by `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` when you call Create Grant, not the amount your form collected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Why is DAFpay blocked on some mobile browsers?**">
Browsers may block a popup that opens too long after the donor's click. Prefetch data and keep all work between the click and DAFpay opening under one second.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Fees

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="**How can our platform collect its processing fee?**">
Pass `applicationFeeAmount` on [Create Grant](/api/grants/create). Chariot either deducts it before paying the nonprofit or invoices the nonprofit for it, on the cadence in your agreement.

Create Grant validates the amount against a ceiling set for your fundraising application and returns a `400` above it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Can we add an optional tip?**">
DAF giving cannot be used to give to a for-profit entity, which means tipping the fundraising platform is not allowed.

If a donor has selected DAFpay, you must suppress the option to add a tip.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**How can donors cover fees?**">
Increase the amount you send to [Create Grant](/api/grants/create) to compensate for the processing fee. Raising the amount also raises the fee, so solve for the final amount rather than adding the fee to the original one, and round the result to a whole dollar — DAF providers only grant in dollar increments.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Receiving funds

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="**How do nonprofits receive the money?**">
It depends on the provider. Gifts from Chariot's disbursement partners arrive electronically in the nonprofit's Chariot account. Other providers pay the organization directly, by ACH or check, on their own schedule.

Either way DAFpay does not hold the funds. Authorizing a request, creating the Grant and receiving the money are three separate events.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**What is the DAFpay Network?**">
For nonprofits that have not completed onboarding and verification with Chariot, DAFpay routes payments through a 501(c)(3) named [DAFpay Network](https://www.dafpaynetwork.org/), which then pays the nonprofit less processing fees. This is what lets DAFpay accept gifts for any eligible 501(c)(3) rather than only for nonprofits already set up with Chariot.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**How do nonprofits match a payment to a specific gift?**">
A DAF payment often arrives weeks or months after the donor gave, with little to identify it. DAFpay records two identifiers to close that gap: the Tracking ID, generated by DAFpay and usually passed through in the grant note, and the External Grant ID, generated by the provider. Both are on the Grant, and both appear in the Chariot dashboard.

Match on the Tracking ID where you have it and fall back to the External Grant ID. See [Processing Donations](/guides/dafpay/accept/processing-donations) for what the remittances look like.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Recurring and unintegrated providers

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="**Does Chariot manage recurring Grant schedules?**">
No. A recurring DAFpay gift records that the donor set the gift up monthly; Chariot does not create a Grant each month. The DAF provider runs the schedule, and a donor cancels by logging into their provider's portal, so Chariot does not learn about cancellations. Reconcile the later Donations individually.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**What is an unintegrated DAF provider?**">
A provider DAFpay cannot submit the request to directly. If the feature is enabled, the modal collects the amount and donor details so the nonprofit is notified in real time, and gives the donor a Tracking ID to include in the note when they complete the gift in their provider's portal.

Because the donor leaves your site to finish, Chariot has no visibility into whether the gift was actually made. Do not mark that intent paid.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Operations

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="**Which identifiers should we store?**">
Store the platform transaction ID, workflow session ID, Connect ID, Grant ID, and Donation ID. Also store and expose `trackingId` and `externalGrantId` when present.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Should we use webhooks?**">
Yes, when you need timely state changes. Verify signatures, process events idempotently, and keep a scheduled recovery process for important state.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Can a completed DAF Grant be refunded?**">
DAF funds are controlled by the provider, not the donor. Whether a request can be canceled depends on the provider and how far it has progressed. Do not present a standard refund action.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
