## Payment Method `status` Lifecycle

A [Payment Method](/api/payment-methods/list) is a destination Chariot can pay an organization through. Its `status` says whether Chariot will pay to it.

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `pending_verification` | The destination was submitted through the API and is awaiting Chariot's review |
| `active` | Chariot will pay to this destination |
| `inactive` | Chariot will no longer pay to this destination. `inactive_reason` says why |

Each `type` has its own lifecycle:

| Type | Becomes active | Becomes inactive |
|------|----------------|------------------|
| `chariot_deposit_account` | The organization opens a Chariot Deposit Account | `closed` when the account closes |
| `us_bank_account` | Chariot verifies the account and sets `expires_at` | `expired` when `expires_at` passes, or `undeliverable` after an ACH transfer is returned |
| `mailing_address` | Chariot verifies the address, whether it was submitted with [Create a Payment Method](/api/payment-methods/create) or entered by Chariot. It has no `expires_at` | `undeliverable` after a check is returned or never deposited, `superseded` when a newer address is verified, or `rejected` for a submission Chariot declines |

## Inactive reasons

| Reason | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `undeliverable` | A payment sent to this destination did not reach the organization: a check was returned or never deposited, or an ACH transfer was returned |
| `superseded` | A newer verified mailing address replaced it |
| `rejected` | Chariot declined the submission, or verified a different submission of the same destination |
| `expired` | The destination passed its `expires_at` without renewal |
| `closed` | The Chariot Deposit Account was closed |

## Staying informed

A new payment method emits a `payment_method.created` [event](/api/webhooks), whoever created it, and every status change emits `payment_method.updated`. Subscribe to them to learn when a destination appears, when a submission becomes `active`, or when an active destination becomes `inactive`, or poll [Get Payment Method](/api/payment-methods/get).

By default, [List Payment Methods](/api/payment-methods/list) returns active methods and submissions awaiting verification. Pass `status=inactive` to see the destinations Chariot will no longer pay to, and why.
