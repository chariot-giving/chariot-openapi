---
title: "Sources"
subtitle: "The payers and giving platforms that send you money."
---

A **Source** is a payer — Fidelity Charitable, Benevity, or any other giving platform that sends money to your organization. Every donation and deposit you receive carries a Source ID that tells you where the money came from.

Use Sources to:

- Look up the payer on a donation or deposit.
- Browse the payers and giving platforms Chariot recognizes.

See [The Source object](object) for the full schema.
