---
title: "Deposits"
subtitle: "Inbound money into your Chariot Processing account."
---

A **Deposit** is a single inbound transfer of money into your Chariot Processing account. Every deposit points at the [Source](../sources/overview) that sent it and can link to one or more [Donations](../donations/overview) that account for the funds.

Use Deposits to:

- See what money has landed in your account and when.
- Reconcile the funds against the individual donations they represent.
- Trace a deposit back to the payer, the payment rail (ACH, check, wire, account transfer), and the underlying bank record.

See [The Deposit object](object) for the full schema.
