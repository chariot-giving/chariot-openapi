---
title: "Donations"
subtitle: "Individual gifts of money to a nonprofit organization."
---

A **Donation** represents a single gift from a donor to a nonprofit. Every donation Chariot knows about — whether it flowed through DAFpay, arrived via a connected charitable-giving platform, was captured through mail or the Chariot Data Sync, or was recorded manually — becomes a Donation resource.

Each Donation carries:

- **Amounts.** Gross, net, fee, and the individual gift portion when a donation is part of a larger disbursement.
- **Attribution.** The primary donor and (optionally) a joint donor.
- **Initiation.** Where and how the donor initiated the gift — a platform name, the platform's own identifier, the URL the donor gave from, and any metadata the platform passed along.
- **Settlement.** How the funds are settling to the nonprofit through Chariot.
- **Payment status.** The current state of the donation in its lifecycle, from incoming to received.
- **Reconciliation handles.** An `external_id` you can set to link the donation back to your own systems, and a `metadata` map for anything else.

Donations belong to a `payment_source` when one applies, and roll up into a `deposit` once the funds settle to the nonprofit.

See [The Donation object](object) for the full schema.
