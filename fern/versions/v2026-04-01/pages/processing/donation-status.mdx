A Donation's `payment_status` tells you where the money is, regardless of how the gift was initiated.

| Value | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `INCOMING_TO_CHARIOT` | The funds are on their way into your Chariot account. Chariot usually matches them automatically and marks the gift `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` once the deposit completes; a deposit Chariot cannot match is reconciled in the dashboard. Chariot emails you once the funds are available. |
| `INCOMING_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | The funds are on their way to you directly from the payer, so Chariot cannot see them land. Someone on your team marks the gift received in the dashboard, unless a connected source records the payment first. |
| `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` | The funds arrived in your Chariot account. |
| `RECEIVED_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | Receipt was confirmed outside Chariot, by you or by a connected source. |
| `CANCELED` | The gift will not be paid. |

Payment status is derived by Chariot from the state of the funds, so it is read only over the API.

## Settlement Timestamps

A Donation can read as received before the money is usable. Check the timestamps when your workflow depends on funds being available.

| Field | What it marks |
| --- | --- |
| `created_at` | When Chariot created the Donation record. |
| `settlement.settled_at` | When the deposit settled at the bank. |
| `settlement.deposit_id` | The Deposit the funds arrived in. |

## Retrieving Donations

[Getting Started](/guides/processing/getting-started#step-3-retrieve-donations) shows the List Donations and Get Donation calls. List Donations returns gifts across every connected payment source on the account, pages with `next_page_token`, which you pass back as `page_token` with the same filters, and caps the page size at 100. You can filter by `payment_source_id`, `deposit_id`, `created_at.after`, `created_at.before`, and `dafpay_tracking_id`.

<Warning title="Filtered by Creation Date">
  List Donations filters on `created_at`, not `updated_at`, so it will not show changes to older gifts. Pair it with `donation.updated` events.
</Warning>

## Recording Your Own Status

Properties are the writable part of a Donation, and you retrieve them alongside the rest of the Donation data. To record your own status, for example that a gift has been entered in your CRM, create a property in the Chariot Dashboard and assign it to the Donation. The [FAQ](/guides/processing/faq#how-do-i-add-custom-fields-to-donations) shows the call.
