Retrieve settled donations, create the records in your system, and mark them recorded in Chariot.

## The objects

```
Payment Source          where money arrives (a DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox)
   └── Deposit          one transfer that landed
          ├── Donation  one donor's gift, coded and attributed
          └── Donation
```

A **payment source** is a segregated address: either a unique account and routing number, or a lockbox mailing address. All of them settle into the same account, but each keeps its own tracking so you can tell gifts apart.

A **deposit** usually covers many donations. A DAF sponsor sending a weekly $10,000 transfer covering 50 donors produces one deposit and 50 donations.

A **donation** carries donor name, email and address; gross, net and fee amounts; purpose and donor note; DAF and platform detail; corporate match; and the properties your team configured.

Gross is the gift plus any corporate match. The fee is what the donation platform or third-party processor charged, not a Chariot fee. Net is what your organization received, and deposits reflect net. Book net and keep the deposit ID for reconciliation.

## When gifts appear

Donation data is available as soon as Chariot receives the transfer, before the funds finish settling.

| Payment rail | Time from the payer sending to the gift appearing |
| --- | --- |
| From another Chariot account | Immediate |
| ACH | 1-2 business days |
| Check | 1-3 business days after it reaches the lockbox, plus 3-5 to settle |

That clock starts when the payer sends the money. A DAF sponsor usually takes 1 to 2 weeks after the donor initiates before it does, so a DAFpay gift takes longer end to end. See [funds delivery times](https://help.givechariot.com/getting-started/funds-delivery-times).

## When to record a gift

We recommend waiting until the funds have landed, then creating the gift in your CRM once. That leaves no pledge records to reconcile against payments later.

Funds have landed when the donation has a deposit, or when it arrived some other way and you have marked it received offline.

If your team needs earlier visibility, create the record when the gift is initiated and update it when the funds arrive. Every gift then touches your CRM twice, and some initiated gifts never fund.

## Retrieving donations

Poll for donations on a schedule. A poll reflects what is actually in Chariot; a webhook can be dropped.

### List deposits that settled in your window

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/deposits" />

Filter with `settled_at.after` and `settled_at.before`.

### List the donations on each deposit

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/donations" />

Pass `deposit_id`. You can also filter by `payment_source_id` or `created_at`. Pages hold up to 100 records; use `page_token` to walk the rest.

### Webhooks

Webhooks tell you sooner, but treat them as a prompt to go look rather than a record of what arrived. Deliveries can fail. In production we retry a non-2xx response up to 10 times with backoff and disable the endpoint after 5 days of failures; in sandbox there are no retries at all.

| Event | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| `donation.created` | A gift is recorded in Chariot |
| `donation.updated` | A donation changes, including settling against a deposit |
| `deposit.created`, `deposit.updated` | A transfer lands or changes status |

Subscribe with [Create Event Subscription](/api/event-subscriptions/create). Signature verification is covered in [Webhooks and Events](/api/webhooks).

Use donation events. `grant.created` only fires for gifts initiated through your own donation form, and initiation is not payment.

If you use webhooks, keep the poll running underneath as a backstop.

## Marking a gift recorded

Write the status back so your finance team sees the same state in the dashboard that your integration sees.

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="POST /v1/properties/{id}/assign" />

<Warning title="Use properties/assign">
`PATCH /v1/donations/{id}` is not available to external API keys and returns `404`.
</Warning>

Create a CRM status property with the states your process uses and drive it from your integration. Saved views per status let your team work the exceptions.

Properties also carry your CRM record ID, internal campaign or fund codes, and processing notes.

Team members who edit properties in the dashboard need the **Development** permission.

## Payment status

Every donation carries one of four payment statuses, set from whether its payment source is connected to your account.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Incoming To Chariot` | Money is expected to arrive in your Chariot Account |
| `Received In Chariot` | Money landed in your Chariot Account |
| `Incoming Outside Chariot` | Money is expected to arrive outside Chariot |
| `Received Outside Chariot` | Money arrived outside Chariot, for example a check sent to your office |

Only `Received In Chariot` means the funds are in your Chariot Account and the donation has a deposit. Do not treat an incoming status as banked.

### Gifts that arrive outside Chariot

A donation only gets a deposit when the funds reach your Chariot Account. Gifts from payers you have not linked arrive by their existing route, so those donations stay at `Incoming Outside Chariot` and never get one.

When the money turns up, find the donation using the DAFpay tracking ID, external ID, or the donor and amount, then **click the payment status field on the donations table and select `Received Outside Chariot`**. This is a manual step in the dashboard.

Linking the payer removes the work. Payment sources are linked in the dashboard.

## Before you launch

* [ ] Payment sources linked in the dashboard for each payer you route
* [ ] Donation coding configured in the dashboard: properties, views, policies
* [ ] Retrieval built on a scheduled poll of [List Deposits](/api/deposits/list) and [List Donations](/api/donations/list)
* [ ] If you use webhooks, the poll still runs underneath as a backstop
* [ ] CRM status property created, including a state for gifts received offline
* [ ] Write-back built on [Assign Property](/api/properties/assign)
* [ ] Any webhook handler returns 2xx quickly, is idempotent, and verifies signatures
* [ ] Your team knows `Pending` is not banked
* [ ] Finance aligned on the reconciliation process

Settlement cannot be triggered in sandbox, so confirm that path with a small live gift after go-live. See [sandbox limitations](/guides/nonprofits/sandbox#current-limitations).
