A Connect controls how DAFpay works for your nonprofit. Use the form below to choose your settings, then email the generated request to [implementations@givechariot.com](mailto:implementations@givechariot.com).

Chariot will send you a sandbox Connect ID to use while you build and test. When your integration is ready, we will review it and issue a separate production Connect ID.

Sandbox Connect IDs begin with `test_`.

<Warning>
Use the Connect ID created for your API integration. Your Chariot account also has Connect IDs for the dashboard's no-code button and link, which submit the gift themselves; pointing your form at one of those produces two gifts for one checkout.
</Warning>

<Tip title="Getting DAF prefill to work">
For DAFpay to fill a donor detail automatically, your form must send **nothing** for that field. An empty value counts as answering it, and the donor gets a blank box. See [Send Donor Data](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/donor-data).
</Tip>

## What happens next

Chariot will email your sandbox Connect ID. Use it to build and test your integration, then complete [Test DAFpay before launch](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/before-launch) to request your production Connect ID.

## Change your settings

To update your Connect later, email [implementations@givechariot.com](mailto:implementations@givechariot.com). Tell us whether the change should apply to sandbox, production, or both.
