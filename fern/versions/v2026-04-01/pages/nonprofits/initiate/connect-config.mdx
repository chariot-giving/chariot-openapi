Your Connect ID identifies the DAFpay configuration for your integration. Decide each setting below, then email your choices to [implementations@givechariot.com](mailto:implementations@givechariot.com).

Chariot will send you a sandbox Connect ID to use while you build and test. When your integration is ready, we will review it and issue a separate production Connect ID. Sandbox Connect IDs begin with `test_`.

## Settings to choose

DAFpay can display information collected on your form, fill in missing details from the donor's DAF account, and collect any additional information your organization requires.

| Setting | What to tell us |
| --- | --- |
| Details to show | Which donor details DAFpay should display or collect. |
| Details to require | Which of those details a donor must provide before continuing. |
| Monthly recurring gifts | Whether donors may request a [monthly grant](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/create-grant#recurring-grants) from their DAF. |
| Unintegrated DAF providers | Whether to record an [Unintegrated Grant](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/events#unintegrated-grants) or return the donor to your form to choose another way to give. |
| Close the window on confirmation | Whether DAFpay closes the window immediately after an [Unintegrated Grant](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/events#unintegrated-grants) is confirmed, rather than waiting for the donor to close it. |

## What happens next

Chariot will email your sandbox Connect ID. Use it to build and test your integration, then complete [Test DAFpay before launch](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/before-launch) to request your production Connect ID.

## Change your settings

To update your Connect later, email [implementations@givechariot.com](mailto:implementations@givechariot.com). Tell us whether the change should apply to sandbox, production, or both.
