Load Chariot Connect with the JavaScript bundle or the React package. Both are covered in the [Chariot Connect reference](/guides/dafpay/integrating-dafpay/connect-reference#mount-the-component).

```html
<script src="https://cdn.givechariot.com/chariot-connect.umd.js"></script>
<chariot-connect id="chariot" cid="YOUR_CONNECT_ID"></chariot-connect>
```

Use your sandbox Connect ID in sandbox and your production Connect ID in production. A Connect ID may appear in browser code. An API key may not.

## Placement

We recommend placing DAFpay above your form as an Express Checkout option. This lets donors open DAFpay before typing information Chariot can retrieve from their DAF account. If that placement does not fit your form, place DAFpay next to your other payment methods.

## Content Security Policy

If your site uses CSP, allow the Chariot script and DAFpay frame according to the current [Content Security Policy requirements](/guides/dafpay/integrating-dafpay/csp). Merge the required sources into your existing policy; do not replace the policy with a copied example.

## Styling

Pass one of the built-in themes — `DefaultTheme`, `LightModeTheme`, `LightBlueTheme`, or `GradientTheme` — or register your own from the supported properties. See [Themes](/guides/dafpay/integrating-dafpay/connect-reference#themes). Do not depend on internal DOM or CSS selectors inside the component.
