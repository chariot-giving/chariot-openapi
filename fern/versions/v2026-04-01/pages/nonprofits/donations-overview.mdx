The Gift Processing API retrieves settled donations so you can create the records in your system and mark them recorded in Chariot.

## The objects

```
Payment Source          where money arrives (a DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox)
   └── Deposit          one transfer that landed
          ├── Donation  one donor's gift, coded and attributed
          └── Donation
```

A **payment source** is a segregated address — its own account and routing number, or a lockbox address. They all settle into the same account, but each keeps separate tracking so you can tell gifts apart.

A **deposit** usually covers many donations: a sponsor's weekly $10,000 transfer for 50 donors is one deposit and 50 donations.

A **donation** carries the donor's name, email and address; gross, net and fee; purpose and note; DAF and platform detail; corporate match; and your configured properties.

Book net. Gross includes any corporate match, the fee is the donation platform's or processor's rather than Chariot's, and deposits reflect net. Keep the deposit ID for reconciliation.

## When gifts appear

Donation data is available as soon as Chariot receives the transfer, before the funds finish settling.

| Payment rail | Time from the payer sending to the gift appearing |
| --- | --- |
| From another Chariot account | Immediate |
| ACH | 1-2 business days |
| Check | 1-3 business days after it reaches the lockbox, plus 3-5 to settle |

That clock starts when the payer sends the money, and a DAF sponsor usually takes 1 to 2 weeks after the donor initiates to do that. See [funds delivery times](https://help.givechariot.com/getting-started/funds-delivery-times).

## When to record a gift

Wait until the funds land, then create the gift once. No pledge records to reconcile later.

Funds have landed when the donation has a deposit, or when you've marked it received offline.

If your team needs earlier visibility, record it at initiation and update it on arrival — but every gift then touches your CRM twice, and some initiated gifts never fund.
