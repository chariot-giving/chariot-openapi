<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Do I need to integrate DAFpay if my fundraising platform supports it?">

        No. Enable DAFpay through the platform. Build the Chariot donation sync only if you need a separate path into your CRM or ERP.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I add DAFpay without engineering work?">

        Yes. Use a no-code button or link from the dashboard when you do not need to pass form data into DAFpay or receive the result in your page.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="When should I create the gift in my CRM?">

        Use the rule your finance and fundraising teams follow for recognizing a received gift. The simplest implementation creates the CRM gift once the payment is considered received. If you create a pending gift earlier, you must update it later and handle grants that never fund.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does completing DAFpay mean the nonprofit received the money?">

        No. It means the donor completed the DAFpay flow. The DAF provider still processes and sends the grant.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Is a Grant the same as a Donation?">

        No. A Grant is the request sent to the DAF provider. A Donation is Chariot's gift record. A Deposit is the transfer containing funds.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I update a donation through the API?">

        Donation fields are read-only. Use donation properties to record your processing status or internal identifiers.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Should I use webhooks or polling?">

        Use both when you need timely updates. Webhooks reduce latency; a checkpointed scheduled sync provides recovery.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why does a donation have no deposit?">

        The funds may not have arrived through a payment source connected to your Chariot Account. Retrieve donations directly by time window so these records are not missed.

    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I test settlement in sandbox?">

        Sandbox does not move real funds. Test your handlers with representative payloads, then confirm the end-to-end path with a small production gift.

    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
