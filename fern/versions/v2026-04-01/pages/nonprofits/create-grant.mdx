<Steps>

### Listen for `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`

You get a `grantIntent`, or a `recurringGrantIntent` for a monthly gift.

### Submit your own form

Persist the gift on your side first, so a failed submission can't leave your records out of step with Chariot's.

### Call Create Grant

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="POST /v1/grants" />

Use [Create Recurring Grant](/api/recurring-grants/create) for a `recurringGrantIntent`.

Send the amount from `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`, not from your form. It can change inside the window if the donor hits their fund balance or the provider enforces a minimum.

Send it in whole dollars, meaning cents rounded to the nearest 100, because DAFs only accept whole-dollar grants. If your form total has cents in it, round before you call. The amount from `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` is already whole.

</Steps>

<Warning title="Capture within 15 minutes">
A grant intent expires 15 minutes after authorization. After that, Create Grant returns `410 Gone` and the donor has to start over, so don't put a queue, a batch job, or a manual approval between `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` and this call.
</Warning>

Retrying is safe. Create Grant is idempotent on `workflowSessionId`: the first successful capture returns `201 Created`, and calling it again with the same session returns `200 OK` with the grant that already exists. You get `409 Conflict` only while a capture for that session is still in flight, which means waiting a moment and reading the grant rather than submitting again.

Receiving `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` does not create a grant. If nothing calls Create Grant, the browser flow looks successful and [List Grants](/api/grants/list) returns nothing.

## Capturing the grant is what creates the donation

Create Grant does two things: it submits the grant to the donor's provider and it creates the donation record behind it. Everything you read later through [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations) exists because this call succeeded.

So a failed capture doesn't leave a gift half-recorded. There's no grant and no donation, only a workflow session that was authorized and never used.

## Finding the donation

`donationId` on [Get Grant](/api/grants/get) links a grant to its donation, and that ID is where [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations) picks up.

<Warning title="The donation is created asynchronously">
It doesn't exist yet when Create Grant returns. Over a recent 30-day sample the ID showed up in about half a second at the median and within 1.3 seconds for 95% of grants, but a few took more than 5 seconds and one took close to an hour. Retry Get Grant with a short backoff instead of calling it once.
</Warning>

Don't fall back to matching on the DAFpay tracking ID. About 8% of grants don't have one, so integrations built on it end up duplicating those gifts. The tracking ID is there for recognizing a DAFpay gift on a sponsor's remittance when the funds arrive outside Chariot.

## Recurring gifts

A donor can set up a `MONTHLY` gift, which you capture with [Create Recurring Grant](/api/recurring-grants/create).

The provider owns the schedule. Chariot records the initial grant with `frequency: MONTHLY`, doesn't create a new grant each month, and isn't told if the donor cancels or changes it.

Each monthly payment then arrives as its own donation, with nothing linking it back to the original grant. Group them by donor name, provider, fund name and amount. To tell the first gift of a series from a one-time gift, check `initiation.frequency` on the donation. Otherwise process them the same way.

Turn the monthly option on or off in your [connect setup](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/connect-config).

## Donors on an unconnected provider

DAFpay connects to most large DAF providers, but not all. When a donor picks one we're not connected to, Chariot records the intent and emails the donor instructions to finish the gift in their own portal.

Many of these are never completed, and we have no way to tell which. Don't create a CRM record from an intent. Wait for the donation like any other gift.

Your confirmation page has to show the donor what to do next: the tracking ID with its `Tracking ID:` prefix and a way to copy it, your EIN, your organization name, and a link to their provider. Without those they can't finish the gift.

Choose whether to accept these in your [connect setup](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/connect-config).
