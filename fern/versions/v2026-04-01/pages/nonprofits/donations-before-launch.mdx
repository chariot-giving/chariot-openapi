---
title: Test Donation Retrieval Before Launch
hidden: false
---

Complete this checklist before running the production sync.

- Required payment sources are connected in the dashboard.
- Donation coding and CRM-status properties are configured.
- Finance and fundraising operations agree on when a gift is considered received.
- The scheduled sync uses checkpoints, pagination, overlapping windows, and idempotent processing.
- The integration requests every page when a result contains more than 100 records.
- The sync retrieves Donations both through Deposits and by creation time.
- A Donation returned by both paths is processed only once.
- The sync handles a Donation with no Deposit.
- Webhook signatures are verified and polling remains enabled as recovery.
- Repeated webhook events are safe.
- A partial CRM or ERP failure does not advance the checkpoint or mark the Donation as recorded.
- Property write-back occurs only after the downstream write succeeds.
- The full reconciliation path is confirmed with a small production gift.
