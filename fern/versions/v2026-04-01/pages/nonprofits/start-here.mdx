DAFpay is available on 70+ fundraising platforms. If your donation form is hosted by one of them, turn DAFpay on there. You don't need to integrate with the Chariot API.

<Steps>

### Find your fundraising platform

Search the [DAFpay integrations list](https://www.givechariot.com/dafpay-setup#integration-search). Givebutter, Fundraise Up, Blackbaud, Classy, GoFundMe Pro, Neon One, EveryAction and dozens of others are on it.

### Turn DAFpay on in your platform

Your platform's own help article covers where the switch is, whether it applies per form or account-wide, and how gifts reach your CRM. Your platform already built the Chariot integration, so none of the API work in these guides applies to you.

### Claim your Chariot Deposit Account

Gifts settle into it, and it's where you see them coded in one place.

</Steps>

For nonprofits on a supported platform, those are the only steps required. Nothing below is.

## The no-code button

Claiming your Chariot Deposit Account already gives you a button, a tile and a web link, each with its own Connect ID. They need no engineering: paste the snippet, or link to the web link.

They work well on a Ways to Give page, or a standalone page for one event, where the donor arrives, clicks and gives.

They're a poor fit for your main donation form, for two reasons:

- **Data your form collects is not passed to the gift.** The button passes only the page URL, and the web link passes nothing. A designation, a dedication, a fee-coverage choice, a campaign code or an appeal is lost.
- **The result is not returned to your form.** The gift is submitted inside the window, and when the window closes your form has not been told anything. The donor fills in your fields, clicks, and your page never learns a gift happened.

If you need to pass information into DAFpay, or receive the result in your form, use the API integration below.

## When you do need the API

| Situation | What to read |
| --- | --- |
| Your platform is on the list | Nothing here. Turn DAFpay on with them. |
| You want DAFpay gifts, and every other gift, pulled into your CRM or ERP automatically | [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations) |
| You built and run your own donation form, and pass data with the gift | [Initiate Gifts](/guides/nonprofits/initiate), then [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations) |
| You want DAFpay on a Ways to Give page and nothing more | The button or web link from your dashboard. No code. |
| Your platform isn't on the list | [Talk to us](mailto:support@givechariot.com) before you build anything. There may be a shorter route than an integration. |

## Where gifts appear

Where a DAFpay gift shows up depends on how DAFpay got onto your form.

| How DAFpay is on your form | Gift appears in Chariot | Gift appears in your fundraising platform | Reaches your CRM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Through your fundraising platform | Yes | Yes | Only if that platform has a CRM integration and you use it |
| The no-code button, tile or web link | Yes | No | Only if you build it, through [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations) |
| A form you built, on the API | Yes | Not applicable | However you build it |

If gifts have to land in your CRM, check with your platform before assuming DAFpay rides along. Plenty of platforms show the gift but don't sync it.

If you use the no-code button, tile or web link, you can still pull the resulting donations through the API. Retrieving donations is worth doing however your gifts arrive, and it's independent of who put DAFpay on your form.
