Poll for donations on a schedule. A poll reflects what is actually in Chariot; a webhook can be dropped.

<Steps>

### List deposits that settled in your window

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/deposits" />

Filter with `settled_at.after` and `settled_at.before`.

### List the donations on each deposit

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/donations" />

Pass `deposit_id`. You can also filter by `payment_source_id`, or by `created_at.after` and `created_at.before`. Pages hold up to 100 records; use `page_token` to walk the rest.

Rate limits apply and are returned in the response headers. Watch them when you loop deposits, and contact us if your volume needs more headroom.

### Get the full record

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/donations/{id}" example="GetDonation" />

</Steps>

## Webhooks

Webhooks tell you sooner, but treat them as a prompt to go look rather than a record of what arrived. Deliveries can fail. In production we retry a non-2xx response up to 10 times with backoff and disable the endpoint after 5 days of failures; in sandbox there are no retries at all.

| Event | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| `donation.created` | A gift is recorded in Chariot |
| `donation.updated` | A donation changes, including settling against a deposit |
| `deposit.created`, `deposit.updated` | A transfer lands or changes status |

Subscribe with [Create Event Subscription](/api/event-subscriptions/create). Signature verification is covered in [Webhooks and Events](/api/webhooks).

Subscribe to donation events. `grant.created` only fires for gifts initiated through your own donation form, and initiation is not payment.

If you use webhooks, keep the poll running underneath as a backstop.
