Chariot collects your gifts from every source: DAF sponsors, fundraising platforms, workplace giving, checks. It codes them in one place.

The API moves those coded gifts into your CRM or ERP:

<Steps>

### Retrieve donations

Pull donations with donor attribution and your codes already applied.

### Create the records in your system

Map the fields to your CRM or ERP.

### Mark them recorded

Assign a property back on the donation so Chariot shows the gift as handled.

</Steps>

Start with the [Gift Processing API](/guides/nonprofits/gift-processing).

## How gifts reach Chariot

| Source | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| DAFpay on your fundraising platform | Nothing. They arrive on their own. |
| The DAFpay component on your site | Nothing. They arrive on their own. |
| A linked DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox | Link it once in the dashboard. |
| DAFpay on a donation form you built | Call the Grants API to initiate the gift. |

If you built your own donation form, see [Custom Donation Forms](/guides/nonprofits/dafpay). The Grants API initiates the gift; you work with donations from there.

## Onboarding

<Steps>

### Create your account

Sign up at [givechariot.com](https://givechariot.com) with your organization's EIN. A control person has to claim the account.

### Complete verification

Chariot verifies your nonprofit status and control person. This takes 3-10 business days.

### Link your external bank account

Settings, then External Financial Accounts. Once linked, move funds from the account Overview page with **Move Money**, or set up automatic transfers on a schedule. ACH takes 2-3 business days and only Account Owners can initiate it.

### Set up payment sources

One per DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox, then share your Chariot banking details with each of them.

### Generate an API key

One per environment, generated in the dashboard.

</Steps>

Build against [sandbox](/guides/nonprofits/sandbox) first.
