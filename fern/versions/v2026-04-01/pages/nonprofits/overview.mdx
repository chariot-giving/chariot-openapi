Chariot collects your gifts from every source: DAF sponsors, fundraising platforms, workplace giving, checks. It codes them in one place, and the API moves them into your CRM or ERP.

<Warning title="If you use a fundraising platform, you don't need to integrate DAFpay">
DAFpay is available on 70+ fundraising platforms. If your donation form is hosted by one of them, turn DAFpay on there instead of building against our API. [Find your platform](https://www.givechariot.com/dafpay-setup#integration-search), or see [Do I Need to Integrate?](/guides/nonprofits/start-here).

You would still use the API to pull the gifts you receive into your CRM or ERP. That's [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations), and it applies however DAFpay got onto your form.
</Warning>

## Which APIs you'll use

<Steps>

### Capture the gift

Only if you built and run your own donation form. DAFpay authorizes the donor's gift in the browser, your server calls the Grants API to capture it, and a donation follows from that.

If a fundraising platform hosts your form, you don't need this step. See [Initiate Gifts](/guides/nonprofits/initiate).

### Retrieve donations

Pull donations with donor attribution and your codes already applied, create the records in your system, and mark them recorded in Chariot.

See [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations).

</Steps>

## What each stage means

```
DAFpay session → grant intent → Grant → Donation → Deposit
```

The donor authorizes an intent in the browser. Capturing that intent creates a **Grant**, the donor's instruction to their DAF provider. A **Donation** is the gift record Chariot keeps once the provider acts on it, and a **Deposit** is the transfer the funds arrive in.

A successful DAFpay session means the donor authorized an intent. It doesn't mean a grant exists, and a donation that exists doesn't mean the funds have arrived. These are four separate stages.

## How gifts reach Chariot

| Source | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| DAFpay on your fundraising platform | No API call to start the gift. It reaches Chariot on its own. |
| The DAFpay component on your site | No API call to start the gift. It reaches Chariot on its own. |
| A linked DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox | Link it once in the dashboard, then nothing per gift. |
| DAFpay on a donation form you built | Your server captures each gift with the Grants API. |

Either way you retrieve and record the donations. The difference is whether your own server creates the grant or the gift reaches Chariot another way.
