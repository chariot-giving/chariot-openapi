Chariot has two APIs:

- **DAFpay** adds a DAF payment option to a donation form you own.
- **Gift Processing** brings your donation data into your CRM or ERP.

Use either on its own, or both together. A lot of what Chariot does needs no API at all, so start with the question below.

<Note>
This section is for nonprofits. If you build a fundraising platform that serves many nonprofits, use [Add DAFpay to your fundraising platform](/guides/dafpay/overview) instead — that integration is set up once for every nonprofit you serve, not once for one form.
</Note>

## Do you need an API?

| Your situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A fundraising platform hosts your donation form | Search the [DAFpay integrations list](https://www.givechariot.com/dafpay-setup#integration-search). If your platform is listed, turn DAFpay on there — you build nothing. |
| You want DAFpay on a page you own, quickly | Use the DAFpay component and shareable link in your [Chariot dashboard](https://dashboard.givechariot.com). Both take a few minutes and work well for a Ways to Give or campaign page. |
| Your form needs to exchange data with DAFpay | Integrate DAFpay. Start with [Add DAFpay to a donation form you own](/guides/nonprofits/initiate). |
| You need donation data in your CRM or ERP | Use the Gift Processing API. Start with [Retrieve donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations). |
| You only need donation data on a schedule | Build a custom export template in the dashboard to format it for your CRM or ERP. |

## What an API integration adds

The dashboard component is quick, but it does not receive fields from your donation form and does not report the result back to your page.

Integrate DAFpay when your form needs to:

- pass an amount, designation, donor details, or internal metadata into DAFpay;
- receive the result of the DAFpay session; or
- create and track the grant in your own system.

Use the Gift Processing API to retrieve donations regardless of how they arrived — through DAFpay, a fundraising platform, a linked DAF provider, workplace giving, or a lockbox. It also lets you write back to Chariot after your system processes a gift, which an export cannot do.

## The gift lifecycle

Every page in this section assumes these three records.

| Record   | What it represents                                                                                             |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Grant    | The request sent to the donor's DAF provider through DAFpay. Only gifts initiated through DAFpay have a Grant. |
| Donation | Chariot's record of the gift and its donor, amount, coding, and payment status.                                |
| Deposit  | The transfer in which funds arrive. One deposit can contain multiple donations.                                |

A donor completing DAFpay does not mean the funds have arrived. DAF providers review and send grants on their own timelines.

Gifts that were not initiated through DAFpay do not have a Grant. Once Chariot receives data for one of those gifts, it is represented as a Donation and, when the funds arrive in a Chariot Account, a Deposit.

## Before you begin

Account setup happens in the [Chariot dashboard](https://dashboard.givechariot.com), not through the API. Use the [Help Center](https://help.givechariot.com) to claim and verify your account, connect payment sources, and configure money movement.

Use the dashboard alongside the APIs: the dashboard connects payment sources and configures donation coding, while the API adds DAFpay to a custom form, retrieves donation data, and writes processing status back to Chariot.
