Chariot collects your gifts from every source: DAF sponsors, fundraising platforms, workplace giving, checks. It codes them in one place, and the API moves them into your CRM or ERP.

<Warning title="Most nonprofits should not build a DAFpay integration">
DAFpay is already available on 70+ fundraising platforms. If your donation form is hosted by one of them, turn DAFpay on with your platform rather than building against our API. [Find your platform](https://www.givechariot.com/dafpay-setup#integration-search), or see [Do I Need to Integrate?](/guides/nonprofits/start-here).

You'd still use the API for one thing: pulling the gifts you receive into your CRM or ERP. That's [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations), and it's worth doing however DAFpay got onto your form.
</Warning>

## The two APIs, in order

<Steps>

### Capture the gift

Only if you built and run your own donation form. Skip this if a fundraising platform hosts it. DAFpay authorizes the donor's gift in the browser, and your server calls the Grants API to capture it. A donation follows from that.

Everyone else skips this step. See [Initiate Gifts](/guides/nonprofits/initiate).

### Retrieve donations

Pull donations with donor attribution and your codes already applied, create the records in your system, and mark them recorded in Chariot.

See [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations).

</Steps>

## What each stage means

```
DAFpay session → grant intent → Grant → Donation → Deposit
```

The donor authorizes an intent in the browser. Capturing that intent creates a **Grant**, the donor's instruction to their DAF provider. A **Donation** is the gift record Chariot keeps once the provider acts on it, and a **Deposit** is the transfer the money actually arrives in.

A successful DAFpay session is not a gift, and a donation that exists is not money in the bank. Nothing here can be collapsed into "the donation succeeded".

## How gifts reach Chariot

| Source | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| DAFpay on your fundraising platform | No API call to start the gift. It reaches Chariot on its own. |
| The DAFpay component on your site | No API call to start the gift. It reaches Chariot on its own. |
| A linked DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox | Link it once in the dashboard, then nothing per gift. |
| DAFpay on a donation form you built | Your server captures each gift with the Grants API. |

Either way you still retrieve and record the donations. The difference is only whether you start the gift yourself.
