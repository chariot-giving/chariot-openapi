Write status back so your finance team sees in the dashboard what your integration sees.

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="POST /v1/properties/{id}/assign" />

<Warning title="Use properties/assign">
`PATCH /v1/donations/{id}` is not available to external API keys and returns `404`.
</Warning>

Create a CRM status property with the states your process uses and drive it from your integration; saved views per status let your team work the exceptions. Properties also carry your CRM record ID, campaign or fund codes, and processing notes.

## Payment status

Every donation carries one of four payment statuses, set from whether its payment source is connected to your account.

| `payment_status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `INCOMING_TO_CHARIOT` | Money is expected to arrive in your Chariot Account |
| `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` | Money landed in your Chariot Account |
| `INCOMING_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | Money is expected to arrive outside Chariot |
| `RECEIVED_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | Money arrived outside Chariot, for example a check sent to your office |
| `CANCELED` | The donation will not arrive |

Only `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` means the funds are in your Chariot Account and the donation has a deposit. Do not treat an incoming status as banked.

The dashboard shows friendlier labels for these, so match on the values above rather than on what the screen says.

## Gifts that arrive outside Chariot

A donation only gets a deposit when funds reach your Chariot Account. Gifts from payers you haven't linked arrive by their existing route, so they stay at `INCOMING_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` forever.

When the money turns up, find the donation by tracking ID, external ID, or donor and amount, then set its payment status on the donations table. That's a manual step in the dashboard, and linking the payer removes it.

## Before you launch

* [ ] Payment sources linked in the dashboard for each payer you route
* [ ] Donation coding configured in the dashboard: properties, views, policies
* [ ] Retrieval built on a scheduled poll of [List Deposits](/api/deposits/list) and [List Donations](/api/donations/list)
* [ ] If you use webhooks, the poll still runs underneath as a backstop
* [ ] CRM status property created, including a state for gifts received offline
* [ ] Write-back built on [Assign Property](/api/properties/assign)
* [ ] Any webhook handler returns 2xx quickly, is idempotent, and verifies signatures
* [ ] Your team knows an incoming status is not banked
* [ ] Finance aligned on the reconciliation process

Settlement can't be triggered in sandbox, so confirm that path with a small live gift after go-live. See [sandbox limitations](/guides/nonprofits/sandbox#current-limitations).
