Write the status back so your finance team sees the same state in the dashboard that your integration sees.

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="POST /v1/properties/{id}/assign" />

<Warning title="Use properties/assign">
`PATCH /v1/donations/{id}` is not available to external API keys and returns `404`.
</Warning>

Create a CRM status property with the states your process uses and drive it from your integration. Saved views per status let your team work the exceptions. Properties also carry your CRM record ID, internal campaign or fund codes, and processing notes.

## Payment status

Every donation carries one of four payment statuses, set from whether its payment source is connected to your account.

| `payment_status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `INCOMING_TO_CHARIOT` | Money is expected to arrive in your Chariot Account |
| `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` | Money landed in your Chariot Account |
| `INCOMING_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | Money is expected to arrive outside Chariot |
| `RECEIVED_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | Money arrived outside Chariot, for example a check sent to your office |
| `CANCELED` | The donation will not arrive |

Only `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` means the funds are in your Chariot Account and the donation has a deposit. Do not treat an incoming status as banked.

The dashboard shows friendlier labels for these, so match on the values above rather than on what the screen says.

## Gifts that arrive outside Chariot

A donation only gets a deposit once the funds reach your Chariot Account. Gifts from payers you haven't linked arrive by their existing route, so those donations stay at `INCOMING_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` and never get one.

When the money turns up, find the donation by its tracking ID, external ID, or the donor and amount, then set its payment status on the donations table. That's a manual step in the dashboard, and linking the payer takes it away.

<Info>
Chariot is a financial technology company, not a bank. Chariot Accounts come with a Demand Deposit Account through our banking services partner, Column N.A., Member FDIC. Deposits in Chariot Accounts are eligible for FDIC insurance up to $250,000 per depositor, for each insurable capacity in which the account is held.
