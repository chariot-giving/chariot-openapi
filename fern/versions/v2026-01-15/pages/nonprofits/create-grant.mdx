<Steps>

### Listen for `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`

You get a `grantIntent`, or a `recurringGrantIntent` for a monthly gift.

### Submit your own form

Persist the gift on your side first, so a failed submission can't leave your records out of step with Chariot's.

### Call Create Grant

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="POST /v1/grants" />

Use [Create Recurring Grant](/api/recurring-grants/create) for a `recurringGrantIntent`.

Send the amount from `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`, not from your form. It can change inside the window if the donor hits their fund balance or the provider enforces a minimum.

</Steps>

<Warning title="15 minutes, one attempt">
A grant intent has to be captured within 15 minutes of authorization or the call returns `410 Gone`. Each intent can only be captured once; a duplicate returns `409 Conflict`.

Don't put a queue, a batch job, or a manual approval between `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` and this call. Make the handler idempotent so a retry doesn't attempt a second capture.
</Warning>

Receiving `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` does not create a grant. If nothing calls Create Grant, the browser flow looks successful and [List Grants](/api/grants/list) returns nothing.

## Finding the donation

`donation_id` on [Get Grant](/api/grants/get) links a grant to its donation, and that ID is where [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations) picks up.

<Warning title="The donation is created asynchronously">
It doesn't exist when Create Grant returns. Over a recent 30-day sample the ID appeared in half a second at the median and within 1.3 seconds for 95% of grants, but a few took over 5 seconds and one took nearly an hour. Retry Get Grant with a short backoff rather than calling it once.
</Warning>

Don't match on the DAFpay tracking ID instead. About 8% of grants don't have one, and integrations built on it duplicate those gifts. The tracking ID is for recognizing a DAFpay gift on a sponsor's remittance when funds arrive outside Chariot.

## Recurring gifts

A donor can set up a `MONTHLY` gift, which you capture with [Create Recurring Grant](/api/recurring-grants/create).

The provider owns the schedule. Chariot records the initial grant with `frequency: MONTHLY`, doesn't create a new grant each month, and isn't told if the donor cancels or changes it.

Each monthly payment then arrives as its own donation with nothing linking it to the original grant. Group them by donor name, provider, fund name and amount. Check `initiation.frequency` to tell the first gift of a series from a one-time gift; otherwise process them the same way.

Turn the monthly option on or off in your [connect setup](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/connect-config).

## Donors on an unconnected provider

DAFpay connects to most large DAF providers, but not all. When a donor picks one we're not connected to, Chariot records the intent and emails the donor instructions to finish the gift in their own portal.

Many are never completed and we can't tell which. Don't create a CRM record from an intent — wait for the donation like any other gift.

Your confirmation page has to show the donor what to do next: the tracking ID with its `Tracking ID:` prefix and a way to copy it, your EIN, your organization name, and a link to their provider. Without those they can't finish the gift.

Choose whether to accept these in your [connect setup](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/connect-config).

## Before you launch

* [ ] Connect requested and [configured](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/connect-config) in sandbox and production
* [ ] Chariot Connect installed, Connect ID wired to the component
* [ ] Button at the top of the form, above the amount
* [ ] No empty-string identity fields in `onDonationRequest`, and DAF prefill verified
* [ ] Create Grant called well inside the 15-minute window, and the handler is idempotent
* [ ] Recurring intents branch to Create Recurring Grant
* [ ] Grants created with the amount from `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`
* [ ] One checkout produces one grant
* [ ] `donation_id` read from Get Grant with a retry, not matched on tracking ID
* [ ] Confirmation page renders tracking ID, EIN, organization name, and provider link
* [ ] Popup timing checked on mobile, under 1000ms from click to popup
* [ ] Everything downstream built on [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations)
