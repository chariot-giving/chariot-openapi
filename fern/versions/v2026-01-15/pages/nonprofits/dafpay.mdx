If DAFpay runs on your fundraising platform or through the DAFpay component, gifts arrive in Chariot on their own and you do not need this page.

This is for teams running DAFpay inside a donation form they built. The Grants API initiates the gift. Everything after that is the [Gift Processing API](/guides/nonprofits/gift-processing).

## What you need

Your dashboard API key already covers grants and donations, so you do not need a second one.

You do need a **connect**, your configured instance of DAFpay, which produces the Connect ID (CID) that initializes the button. Chariot creates connects, so [request yours](/guides/nonprofits/dafpay/connect-config) and we set up sandbox and production together.

Button installation, theming, popup handling, the `onDonationRequest` schema, and exit reasons are in [Integrating Connect](/guides/dafpay/integrating-dafpay/integration).

## Initiating the gift

<Steps>

### Listen for `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`

You get a `grantIntent`, or a `recurringGrantIntent` for a monthly gift.

### Submit your own form

Persist the gift on your side first, so a failed submission cannot leave your records out of step with Chariot's.

### Call Create Grant

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="POST /v1/grants" />

Use [Create Recurring Grant](/api/recurring-grants/create) for a `recurringGrantIntent`.

Send the amount from `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`, not from your form. It can change inside the window if the donor hits their fund balance or the provider enforces a minimum.

</Steps>

<Warning title="15 minutes, one attempt">
A grant intent has to be captured within 15 minutes of authorization or the call returns `410 Gone`. Each intent can only be captured once; a duplicate returns `409 Conflict`.

Do not put a queue, a batch job, or a manual approval between `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` and this call. Make the handler idempotent so a retry does not attempt a second capture.
</Warning>

Receiving `CHARIOT_SUCCESS` does not create a grant. If nothing calls Create Grant, the browser flow looks successful and [List Grants](/api/grants/list) returns nothing.

## Finding the donation

`donation_id` on [Get Grant](/api/grants/get) links a grant to its donation.

<Warning title="The donation is created asynchronously">
It does not exist when Create Grant returns, so `donation_id` is not in that response.

Across a recent 30-day sample it appeared in about half a second at the median and within 1.3 seconds for 95% of grants, but 20 took longer than 5 seconds and one took close to an hour. Every grant eventually got one. Retry Get Grant with a short backoff rather than calling it once.
</Warning>

Do not match grants to donations on the DAFpay tracking ID. About 8% of grants do not have one and the field comes back null, so integrations built on it create duplicate records for the gifts that are missing it. Every grant has a `donation_id`.

The tracking ID travels with the money, so it helps you recognize a DAFpay gift on a sponsor's remittance when funds arrive outside Chariot.

Once you have the donation ID, continue in the [Gift Processing API](/guides/nonprofits/gift-processing).

## Recurring gifts

A donor can set up a `MONTHLY` gift from their DAF.

The DAF provider owns the schedule. Chariot records the initial grant with `frequency: MONTHLY` and does not create a new grant each month, and we are not notified if the donor cancels or changes it.

Each monthly payment arrives as its own donation from the linked payer, with nothing linking it back to the original grant. Group them by donor name, DAF provider, fund name, and amount.

To tell the first gift of a series from a one-time gift, check `initiation.frequency` on the donation. Otherwise process them the same way.

Turn the monthly option on or off in your [connect setup](/guides/nonprofits/dafpay/connect-config).

## Donors whose DAF provider is not connected

DAFpay connects to most large DAF providers, but not all. When a donor picks one we are not connected to, Chariot records the intent and emails the donor instructions to finish the gift in their own portal.

Many of these are never completed, and we have no way to know when one is. Do not create a CRM gift record from an intent. Wait for the donation like any other gift.

Your confirmation page has to show the donor what to do next: the tracking ID including its `Tracking ID:` prefix with a way to copy it, your EIN, your organization name, and a link to their DAF provider. Without those the donor cannot complete the gift.

Choose whether to accept these in your [connect setup](/guides/nonprofits/dafpay/connect-config).

## Common mistakes

**Empty strings block DAF prefill.** DAFpay fills the donor's name, email, phone, and address from their DAF account for any field you do not send. Chariot treats any value you pass in `onDonationRequest` as provided, including an empty string, so sending `email: ""` means the donor types it by hand. Omit those keys entirely and send only what we cannot get from the provider: campaign and source IDs, a designation, and an amount if you want to force one.

**Button placement changes what you raise.** Put the button at the top of your form, after currency and one-time versus monthly and above the gift amount. Prefill only works when the button comes before your contact fields, and if you pass no `amount`, DAFpay recommends one from the donor's fund balance, which drives larger gifts.

## Before you launch

* [ ] Connect requested and [configured](/guides/nonprofits/dafpay/connect-config) in sandbox and production
* [ ] Chariot Connect installed, Connect ID wired to the component
* [ ] Button at the top of the form, above the amount
* [ ] No empty-string identity fields in `onDonationRequest`, and DAF prefill verified
* [ ] Create Grant called well inside the 15-minute window, and the handler is idempotent
* [ ] Recurring intents branch to Create Recurring Grant
* [ ] Grants created with the amount from `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`
* [ ] One checkout produces one grant
* [ ] `donation_id` read from Get Grant with a retry, not matched on tracking ID
* [ ] Confirmation page renders tracking ID, EIN, organization name, and provider link
* [ ] Popup timing checked on mobile, under 1000ms from click to popup
* [ ] Everything downstream built on the [Gift Processing API](/guides/nonprofits/gift-processing)
