Complete this checklist before running your production sync.

* [ ] Required payment sources are connected in the dashboard.
* [ ] Donation coding and CRM-status properties are configured.
* [ ] The scheduled sync uses checkpoints, pagination, and idempotent processing.
* [ ] The sync retrieves both deposits and donations by time window.
* [ ] Webhook signatures are verified and polling remains enabled as recovery.
* [ ] Property write-back occurs only after the downstream write succeeds.
* [ ] Finance and fundraising operations agree on when a gift is considered received.
* [ ] The full reconciliation path will be confirmed with a small production gift.
