Use a scheduled sync to retrieve new donations. If you also use webhooks, the scheduled sync can pick up anything your webhook endpoint missed.

## A reliable sync pattern

<Steps>

### Read from the last successful checkpoint to the current time

### Request every page in the window

### Process each record idempotently using the Chariot donation ID

### Advance the checkpoint only after the entire window succeeds

Keep a small overlap between windows and deduplicate by ID.

</Steps>

Use this as a starting point and adapt it to your system. The important part is keeping a checkpoint and requesting every page so a failed job does not leave a permanent gap.

## Retrieve deposits and their donations

Use List Deposits for the settlement window, then List Donations with each `deposit_id` to retrieve the gifts in the transfer.

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/deposits" />

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/donations" />

Follow `next_page_token` until no token is returned. Do not process pages concurrently unless your implementation preserves ordering, rate limits, and checkpoint safety.

## Retrieve donations by creation time

Run a donation query by `created_at` as a second pass. This catches donation records that are not associated with a Chariot deposit, including gifts received through a route your organization has not connected to its Chariot Account.

## Webhooks

Subscribe to donation and deposit events if you need updates sooner than your scheduled sync runs. Verify the signature, then retrieve the current Donation or Deposit from the API. Process repeated events safely; Chariot may send the same event more than once.

| Event | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| `donation.created` | A gift is recorded in Chariot |
| `donation.updated` | A donation changes, including settling against a deposit |
| `deposit.created`, `deposit.updated` | A transfer lands or changes status |

Subscribe with [Create Event Subscription](/api/event-subscriptions/create). Signature verification is covered in [Webhooks and Events](/api/webhooks).

Use Donation events for your CRM sync. This gives you one workflow for DAFpay gifts and gifts received from other sources.
