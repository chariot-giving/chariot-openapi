Chariot collects your gifts from every source: DAF sponsors, fundraising platforms, workplace giving, checks. It codes them in one place, and the API moves them into your CRM or ERP.

## The two APIs, in order

<Steps>

### Initiate the gift

Only if you built your own donation form. The Grants API records the donor's DAF gift at checkout, and a donation follows from it.

Everyone else skips this step. See [Initiate Gifts](/guides/nonprofits/initiate).

### Retrieve donations

Pull donations with donor attribution and your codes already applied, create the records in your system, and mark them recorded in Chariot.

See [Retrieve Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations).

</Steps>

## How gifts reach Chariot

| Source | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| DAFpay on your fundraising platform | Nothing. They arrive on their own. |
| The DAFpay component on your site | Nothing. They arrive on their own. |
| A linked DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox | Link it once in the dashboard. |
| DAFpay on a donation form you built | Call the Grants API to initiate the gift. |
