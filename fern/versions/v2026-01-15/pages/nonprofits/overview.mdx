Chariot helps nonprofits accept more DAF gifts and simplify how they are processed. Use [DAFpay](/v2026-01-15/api/grants/create) to accept DAF grant requests directly on your website, and [Gift Processing](/v2026-01-15/api/donations/list) to bring payments and data together, automate coding and reconciliation, and sync gifts to your CRM or accounting system.

<Note>
This guide is for nonprofits using Chariot. If you are a fundraising platform serving multiple nonprofits, see [Fundraising Platforms](/guides/dafpay/overview).
</Note>

## Choose the right setup

### If you do not need a custom integration

If your donation form is hosted by a [supported fundraising platform](https://www.givechariot.com/dafpay-setup#integration-search), enable DAFpay through that platform.

To add DAFpay to a dedicated [Donor Advised Fund giving page](https://help.givechariot.com/dafpay/ways-to-give-page), use the button available in your Chariot dashboard.

### If you want to use the APIs

| What you want to do | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Embed DAFpay into a custom donation flow | [Add DAFpay](/guides/nonprofits/initiate) |
| Process Chariot Donations in your CRM or accounting system | [Process Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations) |

The DAFpay integration uses the Create Grant API. Gift Processing starts with the List Donations API.

## How Chariot records a gift

| Record | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| Grant | A request sent to a donor's DAF provider through DAFpay. Only gifts initiated through DAFpay have a Grant. |
| Donation | Chariot's record of a gift, including its donor, amount, coding, source, and payment status. A Donation can exist with or without a Grant or Deposit. |
| Deposit | A transfer into a Chariot Account. One Deposit can contain funds for multiple Donations. |

## Before you begin

Every organization must claim its Chariot account and complete compliance verification before using Chariot. A control person can complete these steps in the [Chariot dashboard](https://dashboard.givechariot.com/) by following the [Onboarding Guide](https://help.givechariot.com/onboarding/onboarding-guide).

Next, choose the guide that matches your goal:

- [Add DAFpay](/guides/nonprofits/initiate)
- [Process Donations](/guides/nonprofits/donations)
