Chariot helps nonprofits accept DAF gifts and manage gift processing in one place. Bring payments and data together, automate manual work, and send clean records to the systems your team already uses.

<Note>
  This guide is for nonprofits using Chariot. If you are a fundraising platform that serves multiple nonprofits, use [Add DAFpay to your fundraising platform](/guides/dafpay/overview) instead.
</Note>

## Choose the right setup

| What you want to do | Recommended approach |
| --- | --- |
| Enable DAFpay on a form hosted by your fundraising platform | Search the [DAFpay integrations list](https://www.givechariot.com/dafpay-setup#integration-search). If your platform is listed, enable DAFpay through the platform. You do not need to build an integration. |
| Add DAFpay to a page you own without changing your donation form | Add a DAFpay button or shareable link from your [Chariot dashboard](https://dashboard.givechariot.com). This works well for a [Ways to Give page](https://help.givechariot.com/dafpay/ways-to-give-page) or campaign page. |
| Add DAFpay directly to a donation form you own | [Integrate DAFpay](/guides/nonprofits/initiate). Your form can pass the amount, designation, donor details, and internal metadata into DAFpay, then receive the result and create the grant from your server. |
| Bring Chariot donations into your CRM or ERP | Use the [Gift Processing API](/guides/nonprofits/donations). It retrieves donations from every source connected to Chariot and lets your system record its processing status in Chariot. |
| Format donation data for import without building an API integration | Create a [custom export template](https://help.givechariot.com/gift-processing/export-templates) in the dashboard for your CRM or ERP. |

You can use DAFpay, Gift Processing, or both. Adding DAFpay to your form does not require you to build a Gift Processing integration, and using Gift Processing does not require you to integrate DAFpay.

## How Chariot records a gift

The API guides refer to three related records:

| Record | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| Grant | A request sent to a donor's DAF provider through DAFpay. Only gifts initiated through DAFpay have a Grant. |
| Donation | Chariot's record of a gift, including its donor, amount, coding, source, and payment status. A Donation can exist with or without a Grant or Deposit. |
| Deposit | A transfer into a Chariot Account. One Deposit can contain funds for multiple Donations. |

A donor completing DAFpay authorizes the grant request, but it does not mean your organization has received the funds. The DAF provider still needs to review and send the grant.

Gifts received through other sources do not have a Grant. Chariot records them as Donations when the gift data becomes available. If the funds arrive in a Chariot Account, the Donation is also associated with a Deposit.

## Before you begin

Before your organization can use Chariot, a control person must claim the account and complete [compliance verification](https://help.givechariot.com/onboarding/compliance-verification) in the [Chariot dashboard](https://dashboard.givechariot.com). Follow the [Onboarding Guide](https://help.givechariot.com/onboarding/onboarding-guide) for step-by-step instructions. Once the account is approved, you can [connect payment sources](https://help.givechariot.com/getting-started/payment-sources) and [set up transfers](https://help.givechariot.com/account-management/money-movement) based on how your organization plans to use Chariot.

Next, choose the guide that matches your goal:

- [Add DAFpay to a donation form you own](/guides/nonprofits/initiate)
- [Sync donations to your CRM or ERP](/guides/nonprofits/donations)
