The Gift Processing API retrieves settled donations so you can create the records in your system and mark them recorded in Chariot.

## The objects

```
Payment Source          where funds are received (a DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox)
   └── Deposit          one transfer received
          ├── Donation  one donor's gift, coded and attributed
          └── Donation
```

A **payment source** is a segregated address, either its own account and routing number or a lockbox address. They all settle into the same account, but each one keeps separate tracking so you can tell gifts apart.

A **deposit** usually covers many donations. A sponsor's weekly $10,000 transfer covering 50 donors comes through as one deposit and 50 donations.

A **donation** carries the donor's name, email and address; gross, net and fee; purpose and note; DAF and platform detail; corporate match; and your configured properties.

Deposits reflect the net amount. Gross includes any corporate match, and the fee is whatever the donation platform or processor charged rather than a Chariot fee. Keep the deposit ID to reconcile a transfer against the donations in it.

## When gifts appear

Donation data is available as soon as Chariot receives the transfer, before the funds finish settling.

| Payment rail | Time from the payer sending to the gift appearing |
| --- | --- |
| From another Chariot account | Immediate |
| ACH | 1-2 business days |
| Check | 1-3 business days after it reaches the lockbox, plus 3-5 to settle |

The times above start when the payer sends the funds. A DAF sponsor usually takes 1 to 2 weeks after the donor initiates the gift to do that. See [funds delivery times](https://help.givechariot.com/getting-started/funds-delivery-times).

## When to record a gift

Wait until the funds land, then create the gift in your CRM once. That way you have no pledge records to reconcile against payments later.

Funds have landed when the donation has a deposit, or when you've marked it received offline.

If your team needs earlier visibility, record the gift at initiation and update it when the funds arrive. Every gift then touches your CRM twice, and some initiated gifts never fund at all.
