The Gift Processing API retrieves settled donations so you can create the records in your system and mark them recorded in Chariot.

## The objects

```
Payment Source          where money arrives (a DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox)
   └── Deposit          one transfer that landed
          ├── Donation  one donor's gift, coded and attributed
          └── Donation
```

A **payment source** is a segregated address: either a unique account and routing number, or a lockbox mailing address. All of them settle into the same account, but each keeps its own tracking so you can tell gifts apart.

A **deposit** usually covers many donations. A DAF sponsor sending a weekly $10,000 transfer covering 50 donors produces one deposit and 50 donations.

A **donation** carries donor name, email and address; gross, net and fee amounts; purpose and donor note; DAF and platform detail; corporate match; and the properties your team configured.

Gross is the gift plus any corporate match. The fee is what the donation platform or third-party processor charged, not a Chariot fee. Net is what your organization received, and deposits reflect net. Book net and keep the deposit ID for reconciliation.

## When gifts appear

Donation data is available as soon as Chariot receives the transfer, before the funds finish settling.

| Payment rail | Time from the payer sending to the gift appearing |
| --- | --- |
| From another Chariot account | Immediate |
| ACH | 1-2 business days |
| Check | 1-3 business days after it reaches the lockbox, plus 3-5 to settle |

That clock starts when the payer sends the money. A DAF sponsor usually takes 1 to 2 weeks after the donor initiates before it does, so a DAFpay gift takes longer end to end. See [funds delivery times](https://help.givechariot.com/getting-started/funds-delivery-times).

## When to record a gift

Wait until the funds have landed, then create the gift in your CRM once. That leaves no pledge records to reconcile against payments later.

Funds have landed when the donation has a deposit, or when it arrived some other way and you have marked it received offline.

If your team needs earlier visibility, create the record when the gift is initiated and update it when the funds arrive. Every gift then touches your CRM twice, and some initiated gifts never fund.
