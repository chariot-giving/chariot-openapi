The first section covers what an integration needs. The second covers the banking and payment source setup required to receive and reconcile funds, and your finance team can work through it in parallel.

## To integrate

<Steps>

### Create your account

Sign up at [givechariot.com](https://givechariot.com) with your organization's EIN. A control person has to claim the account.

### Complete verification

Chariot verifies your nonprofit status and control person. This takes 3-10 business days, and you can build against [sandbox](/guides/nonprofits/sandbox) while you wait.

### Generate an API key

One per environment, generated in the dashboard. Team members who edit properties in the dashboard need the **Development** permission.

</Steps>

## To receive the money

<Steps>

### Link your external bank account

Settings, then External Financial Accounts. Once linked, move funds from the account Overview page with **Move Money**, or set up automatic transfers on a schedule. ACH takes 2-3 business days, and only Account Owners can initiate it.

### Set up payment sources

One per DAF sponsor, platform, or lockbox, then share your Chariot banking details with each of them. Gifts from payers you haven't linked still appear as donations, but they aren't associated with a deposit.

</Steps>

Next: build against [sandbox](/guides/nonprofits/sandbox).

<Info>
Chariot is a financial technology company, not a bank. Chariot Accounts come with a Demand Deposit Account through our banking services partner, Column N.A., Member FDIC. Deposits in Chariot Accounts are eligible for FDIC insurance up to $250,000 per depositor, for each insurable capacity in which the account is held.
</Info>
