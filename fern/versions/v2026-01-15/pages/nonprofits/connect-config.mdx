A **connect** is your DAFpay configuration, and it controls what the window does when a donor opens it from your form. Chariot creates connects, so make your choices below and send them to us. We set up sandbox and production together, each with its own Connect ID (CID).

## Use the right Connect ID

You may end up holding more than one. Claiming your Chariot Deposit Account gives you a Connect ID for the no-code button, tile and web link, and the connect you request here gives you another for your own form.

They aren't interchangeable. The no-code one submits the gift from inside the window, so pointing your form at it means the window submits a grant and your backend submits another, which is how one checkout produces two gifts. Use the Connect ID we issue for your integration, and ask [support@givechariot.com](mailto:support@givechariot.com) if you're not sure which you have.

Anything you leave at its default is fine. Copy the result, or send it in one click.

<Tip title="Getting DAF prefill to work">
For DAFpay to fill a donor detail automatically, your form must send **nothing** for that field. An empty value counts as answering it, and the donor gets a blank box. See [Send Donor Data](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/donor-data).
</Tip>

<Note title="Changing your setup later">
Send the same request again with what you want changed. Sandbox and production are separate configurations, so say whether a change applies to one or both. We apply it to both unless you tell us otherwise.
</Note>

Before you send it, work through the [pre-launch checklist](/guides/nonprofits/before-launch). We test your form against it.
