Your Connect ID identifies the DAFpay configuration for your nonprofit. Use the form below to choose your settings, then email the generated request to [implementations@givechariot.com](mailto:implementations@givechariot.com).

Chariot will send you a sandbox Connect ID to use while you build and test. When your integration is ready, we will review it and issue a separate production Connect ID.

Sandbox Connect IDs begin with `test_`.

## What happens next

Chariot will email your sandbox Connect ID. Use it to build and test your integration, then complete [Test DAFpay before launch](/guides/nonprofits/initiate/before-launch) to request your production Connect ID.

## Change your settings

To update your Connect later, email [implementations@givechariot.com](mailto:implementations@givechariot.com). Tell us whether the change should apply to sandbox, production, or both.
