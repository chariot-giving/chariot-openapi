Write the status back so your finance team sees the same state in the dashboard that your integration sees. This takes three calls, and the first two only once.

<Steps>

### Create the property in the dashboard

Properties are created in the dashboard, not through the API. Make a CRM status property with the states your process uses, plus a state for gifts you received offline. Team members who edit properties need the **Development** permission.

### Find its ID and its option IDs

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="GET /v1/properties" />

Filter by `resource_type` to get the donation properties. Each one comes back with an `id` like `prop_01j8rs…` and, for a status property, its first 25 `options`. If `total_option_count` is higher than that, page through them with [List Property Options](/api/properties/list-options).

Store the property ID and the option ID for each state. Neither changes.

### Assign the value to donations

<EndpointRequestSnippet endpoint="POST /v1/properties/{id}/assign" />

The `{id}` in the path is the **property** ID, not the donation. The donations go in the body:

```json
{
  "value": { "type": "ENUM", "enum_value_id": "propopt_01j8rs605a4gctmbm58d87mvsj" },
  "resources": { "ids": ["donation_01j8rs605a4gctmbm58d87mvsj"] }
}
```

You can assign one value to up to 100 donations per call, so batch a poll's worth rather than calling once per gift. Passing `"empty": true` in `value` clears the property instead.

</Steps>

Saved views per status let your team work the exceptions. Properties also carry your CRM record ID, internal campaign or fund codes, and processing notes.

## Payment status

Every donation carries one of four payment statuses, set from whether its payment source is connected to your account.

| `payment_status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `INCOMING_TO_CHARIOT` | Money is expected to arrive in your Chariot Account |
| `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` | Money landed in your Chariot Account |
| `INCOMING_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | Money is expected to arrive outside Chariot |
| `RECEIVED_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` | Money arrived outside Chariot, for example a check sent to your office |
| `CANCELED` | The donation will not arrive |

Only `RECEIVED_IN_CHARIOT` means the funds are in your Chariot Account and the donation has a deposit. Do not treat an incoming status as banked.

The dashboard shows friendlier labels for these, so match on the values above rather than on what the screen says.

## Gifts that arrive outside Chariot

A donation only gets a deposit once the funds reach your Chariot Account. Gifts from payers you haven't linked arrive by their existing route, so those donations stay at `INCOMING_OUTSIDE_CHARIOT` and never get one.

When the money turns up, find the donation by its tracking ID, external ID, or the donor and amount, then set its payment status on the donations table. That's a manual step in the dashboard, and linking the payer takes it away.
