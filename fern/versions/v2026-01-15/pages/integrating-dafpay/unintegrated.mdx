---
title: "Unconnected DAF Providers"
subtitle: "Donors whose provider is not integrated with DAFpay"
hidden: false
---

An unintegrated grant is a donor's intent to give from a provider DAFpay isn't connected to. It's a separate object from a grant, with its own status: `Unknown`, `Completed` or `Canceled`. New ones are `Unknown`, since nobody can tell yet whether the donor finished in their provider's portal.

Read them with [List Unintegrated Grants](/api/unintegrated-grants/list), and trigger the flow on the [demo page](https://secure.dafpay.com/demo) by selecting The Boston Foundation.

<Warning title="An intent is not revenue">
Many of these are never completed, and we can't tell which. Don't count an unintegrated grant toward a nonprofit's totals, or show it as a received gift, until it has a donation behind it. If your product surfaces it at all, label it pending or offline.
</Warning>

Chariot moves the status off `Unknown`, not you. It becomes `Completed` when we match an arriving payment to the intent, which only happens if the donor actually finished the gift in their provider's portal. Unintegrated grants are read-only over the API, so subscribe to `unintegrated_grant.updated` if you want to reflect the change on your side.

You can opt out of these. Tell us, and DAFpay sends those donors back to your form to choose another payment method.

If they're enabled, the window closes once the donor enters their details and fires `CHARIOT_EXIT` with reason `UNINTEGRATED_GRANT_CONFIRMED`. That's your cue to record the intent.

<Warning title="The confirmation page has to carry the instructions">
Render what `CHARIOT_EXIT` gives you: the tracking ID, the nonprofit's EIN and name, and a link to the DAF provider. Include a way to copy the tracking ID with its `Tracking ID:` prefix. Without those, the donor can't complete the gift.
</Warning>
