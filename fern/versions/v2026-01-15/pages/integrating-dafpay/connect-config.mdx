Agree on the production behavior before you build. Chariot applies the same configuration to your sandbox Connects before QA and your production Connects before launch, so your test covers the real flow.

Make your choices below and send them to integrations@givechariot.com. The form assembles the request for you. Send it before you build; your sandbox test form and launch date come later, with [Request production access](/guides/dafpay/integrating-dafpay/prod-keys).

<Tip title="Getting DAF prefill to work">
For DAFpay to fill a donor detail automatically, your form must send **nothing** for that field. An empty value counts as answering it, and the donor gets a blank box. See [Send donation data](/guides/dafpay/integrating-dafpay/donor-data).
</Tip>

## Change it later

To change the configuration after launch, contact [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com). Sandbox and production are separate configurations, so say whether a change applies to one or both.
