Complete the sandbox integration and Chariot review before switching to production.

Email your Chariot contact or [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com) with:

## Technical implementation

- platform name and production domains;
- technical and operational contacts;
- sandbox form URL and test instructions;
- webhook endpoint and subscribed event types;
- supported one-time, recurring, fee, and unintegrated-provider behavior; and
- screenshots or a recording of loading, success, exit, and confirmation states.

## Nonprofit rollout

- nonprofit onboarding and enablement flow;
- launch-backfill plan;
- public help article for nonprofits;
- co-branded Chariot onboarding link; and
- target launch date.

## Production validation

- a participating nonprofit and live form Chariot can use for the production test;
- expected CRM sync and hard or soft credit behavior; and
- the contacts responsible for launch support.

When your agreement includes revenue share, also provide the required billing contacts, ACH information, and tax documentation.

## After approval

1. Store the production API key in your secret manager.
2. Switch to `https://api.givechariot.com`.
3. Use production Connect IDs only.
4. Create production event subscriptions.
5. Complete one small live gift with Chariot and the participating nonprofit.

Confirm that exactly one Grant is created, the platform stores its Chariot identifiers, the nonprofit can find the transaction, CRM behavior matches the agreed rules, and donor-facing communications are correct.
