Complete the sandbox integration and Chariot review before switching to production.

Send your Chariot contact, or [integrations@givechariot.com](mailto:integrations@givechariot.com):

- platform name and production domains;
- technical and operational contacts;
- sandbox test form and test instructions;
- nonprofit onboarding and launch-backfill plan;
- Connect configuration choices;
- webhook endpoint and subscribed event types;
- supported payment, fee, recurring, and unsupported-provider behavior; and
- screenshots or recordings of success, exit, loading, and confirmation states.

Before production keys are issued, also confirm:

- a live form Chariot can use for production QA;
- your public help article for nonprofits;
- the co-branded onboarding link your team will distribute;
- your launch and nonprofit-enablement plan; and
- billing contacts, ACH information, and a W-9 when your agreement includes revenue-share payments.

Chariot will review tenant routing, donor experience, grant creation, retries, fees, and reconciliation support.

## After approval

<Steps>

### Store the production key in your secret manager

### Switch to `https://api.givechariot.com`

### Use production Connect IDs only

### Create production event subscriptions

See [Create an Event Subscription](/api/event-subscriptions/create).

### Run a small live gift with Chariot and a participating nonprofit

</Steps>

Production access is not the end of QA. Confirm that the platform is actually live, the Grant is created, the nonprofit can find the transaction, CRM behavior matches the agreed hard/soft-credit rules, and the donor receives the intended confirmation email.

<Warning>
Never infer the environment from an ID alone. Configure the host, API key, and Connect ID together.
</Warning>
