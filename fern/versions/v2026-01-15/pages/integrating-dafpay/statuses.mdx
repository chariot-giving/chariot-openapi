A grant carries one of three statuses.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Initiated` | The grant request was submitted successfully and the DAF provider is still processing it. Every DAFpay grant from an integrated provider starts here. |
| `Completed` | The nonprofit received the funds and marked the gift received in the Chariot dashboard, having identified the line item by its tracking ID or external grant ID. |
| `Canceled` | The grant will not be paid. |

Match on these values exactly; the dashboard's wording for an action does not always match the value the API returns.

Subscribe to the `grant.updated` [webhook event](/api/webhooks) so you can show the current status on your own dashboard. If you accept unintegrated grants, subscribe to `unintegrated_grant.updated` as well; those are separate objects with their own events.


## Cancellations & Refunds

Donors may have the option to cancel their grants from their Donor Advised Fund portal before disbursement, subject to the specific policies of their DAF provider.
As funds in DAFs are no longer under the donor's direct ownership, the concept of refunds after the money leaves the DAF, does not apply.

Donations made via DAFpay are typically **final and irrevocable** gifts (depending on the specific policies of the DAF provider).

<Info title="Cancellation Procedures">
    Canceled grants come back with status `Canceled` and do not incur any processing fees.
    If you believe a grant should be cancelled, please contact the donor first to confirm the cancellation and then send a request to the Chariot support team.
</Info>
