Production keys do not confirm that the integration is live or working end to end. Review the first live forms and Grants with Chariot.

## Confirm the production flow

* [ ] Complete a small live gift on the production form.
* [ ] Confirm the form used the intended nonprofit's production Connect ID.
* [ ] Confirm exactly one Grant was created.
* [ ] Confirm the platform transaction stores the Chariot Grant ID and, when available, the Donation ID.
* [ ] Check the donor-facing confirmation and email copy.
* [ ] Confirm the nonprofit can find and reconcile the gift.
* [ ] Confirm the CRM integration follows the agreed sync and hard/soft-credit rules.

## Check adoption

After 30 days, review how many eligible nonprofits have enabled DAFpay, how many have completed Grants, and where donors leave the flow. Define each metric before comparing it with Chariot; platforms may count a session differently.

Also check for nonprofits that enabled DAFpay but have no activity. Common causes include DAFpay appearing too late in the form, missing form types, incomplete nonprofit setup, and a rollout that was never finished.

## Keep the integration current

Assign owners for:

- changes to authentication, API versions, Connect configuration, and supported form types;
- updates to the nonprofit help article and onboarding link;
- support escalations involving nonprofit identity, EIN changes, fiscal sponsors, or tenant routing;
- advance communication about account-verification or compliance changes; and
- periodic production testing across the supported browser and device matrix.

Treat a change from opt-in to default-on as a new rollout. Repeat tenant-routing, donor-experience, CRM, and communication checks before enabling existing nonprofits in bulk.
