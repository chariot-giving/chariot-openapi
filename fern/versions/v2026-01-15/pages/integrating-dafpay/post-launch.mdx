Production credentials do not confirm that DAFpay is live or working end to end. Validate the first live form and Grant with Chariot and a participating nonprofit.

## Validate the first live gift

- [ ] Confirm the form used the intended nonprofit's production Connect ID.
- [ ] Confirm exactly one Grant was created.
- [ ] Confirm the platform transaction stores the workflow session, Grant ID, and Donation ID when available.
- [ ] Confirm that loading, confirmation, and email copy appear as expected.
- [ ] Confirm the nonprofit can find and reconcile the gift.
- [ ] Confirm CRM sync and hard or soft credit match the agreed rules.

## Review adoption after 30 days

Measure:

- eligible nonprofits;
- nonprofits that enabled DAFpay;
- donors who opened DAFpay;
- donors who authorized a Grant request;
- production Grants created;
- Grants marked `Completed`; and
- where donors leave the flow.

Define each metric before comparing results with Chariot. For example, a completed DAFpay session means the donor authorized a request, while a `Completed` Grant means receipt was confirmed. Platforms and Chariot may also count sessions or enabled nonprofits differently.

Review nonprofits that enabled DAFpay but have no activity. Common causes include placement late in the form, missing form types, incomplete nonprofit setup, incorrect Connect routing, or a rollout that was never completed.

## Keep the integration current

Document an internal owner for:

- [ ] API versions, authentication, and Connect configuration.
- [ ] Supported form types and browser testing.
- [ ] Nonprofit help content and onboarding links.
- [ ] Account, EIN, fiscal-sponsor, and tenant-routing escalations.
- [ ] Compliance and verification changes.
- [ ] Regular production testing.

Treat a change from opt-in to default-on as a new rollout. Repeat tenant-routing, donor-experience, CRM, and communication checks before enabling existing nonprofits in bulk.
