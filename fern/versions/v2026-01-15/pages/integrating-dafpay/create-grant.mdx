## Sequence

<Steps>

### Receive the workflow session and returned amount

### Resolve the nonprofit and platform transaction from your stored session record

### Save your transaction

### Call Create Grant with the server-side API key

### Store the Chariot grant ID and response

</Steps>

<Warning>
Create the grant within 15 minutes of authorization.
</Warning>

## Retry safely

Create Grant is idempotent for the workflow session:

| Response | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `201 Created` | The grant was created. |
| `200 OK` | The grant already existed and was returned. |
| `409 Conflict` | Creation is still in progress; wait and retrieve it. |
| `410 Gone` | The authorization expired; restart DAFpay. |

### A retry, step by step

Your first call times out and you do not know whether the grant exists.

1. Call Create Grant again with the same `workflowSessionId`. Never mint a new one — that is how one authorization becomes two grants.
2. `200 OK` means it already existed. Store the grant it returns and stop.
3. `409 Conflict` means creation is still running. Wait, then call again.
4. `410 Gone` means the 15 minutes elapsed. No grant exists and none will; send the donor back through DAFpay.

Use the amount returned by `CHARIOT_SUCCESS`. Validate that it is a whole-dollar amount.

## Provider limits

Two of the three reasons Create Grant returns `400 Bad Request` come from the
donor's provider, not from your request:

- the amount is below that provider's minimum grant amount; or
- the amount is above the donor's DAF account balance.

Both belong to the fund, so you know them once you know the fund — either
because you preselected `fundId`, or after the donor chooses their provider.
Pass the `fundId` to [Get DAF](/api/donor-advised-funds/get) to read
`minimumGrantAmount`, and `institutionDown` to see whether that provider is
currently unavailable.

Surface a minimum as a message the donor can act on. "This provider requires a
minimum grant of $50" lets them raise the amount; a generic failure does not.

## Platform fees

Use `applicationFeeAmount` only for the platform fee defined in your Chariot agreement — that agreement governs your rate. It is denominated in cents. Chariot's fee and `applicationFeeAmount` are capped together, and exceeding the cap returns `400 Bad Request`; see [Create Grant](/api/grants/create) for the limit the API enforces.

A donor-covered fee increases the grant amount; it is not a separate tip. Do not offer a for-profit platform tip in a DAF transaction.
