---
title: "Mail Items"
subtitle: "Individual pieces of physical mail received at your Lockboxes."
---

A **Mail Item** is a single piece of physical mail received at one of your [Lockboxes](../lockboxes/overview). Each Mail Item carries the scans of what arrived — the envelope, the check, any accompanying paperwork — and can produce one or more [Donations](../donations/overview) and a [Deposit](../deposits/overview).

Use Mail Items to:

- Track what physical mail you've received and its review status.
- Access the scanned images of the mail piece.
- Trace a Donation or Deposit back to the piece of mail it came from.

See [The Mail Item object](object) for the full schema.
