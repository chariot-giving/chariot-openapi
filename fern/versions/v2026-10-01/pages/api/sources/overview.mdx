---
title: "Sources"
subtitle: "The providers and platforms sending you gifts."
---

A **Source** is the provider or platform responsible for facilitating a payment to you and providing access to its data — Fidelity Charitable, Benevity, or any other giving platform that sends you money and records what the gift was for. If you have a question about a gift, the Source is who to contact.

Every donation and deposit you receive carries a Source ID that tells you where it came from.

Use Sources to:

- Look up the provider behind a donation or deposit.
- Browse the providers and platforms Chariot recognizes.

See [The Source object](object) for the full schema.
