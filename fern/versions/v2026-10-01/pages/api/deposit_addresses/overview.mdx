---
title: "Deposit Addresses"
subtitle: "Where [Deposits](../deposits/overview) land in your Chariot Processing account."
---

A **Deposit Address** is a set of bank details — account number and routing number — that receives money into your Chariot Processing account.

Each address can be assigned to a specific [Source](../sources/overview) so incoming funds are attributed automatically, or left unassigned as a catch-all.

Use Deposit Addresses to:

- Give a payer the exact account and routing numbers they should send money to.
- Segregate incoming payments by source without inspecting who sent what after the fact.

See [The Deposit Address object](object) for the full schema.
